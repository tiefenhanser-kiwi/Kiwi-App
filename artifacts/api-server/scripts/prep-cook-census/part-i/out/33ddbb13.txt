    footer 66 min | scheduler 66 | card 66 | fresh derive 66 | dishes 1
      1. [ T-66] (  8m prep) 
         In a large bowl, combine the 12 oz sweet Italian sausage (casings removed), 4 oz ground pork, 1 lightly beaten egg, ⅓ cup breadcrumbs, 3 tablespoons whole milk, the 2 oz finely grated Parmigiano-Reggiano, 2 minced garlic cloves, 2 tablespoons chopped parsley, 1 teaspoon kosher salt, and ½ teaspoon black pepper; mix gently until just combined.
      2. [ T-58] ( 10m prep) 
         Roll the meat mixture into balls about 1 inch in diameter (you should get roughly 28–32) and arrange them on a rimmed baking sheet.
      3. [ T-48] (  8m prep) 
         Finely dice the yellow onion, 2 celery stalks, and 2 carrots; thinly slice the 3 remaining garlic cloves.
      4. [ T-40] (  2m prep) 
         Measure 8 cups low-sodium chicken broth into a large liquid measuring cup or bowl, and add the Parmigiano-Reggiano rind (optional) to the cup so it's ready to go in.
      5. [    —] (  7m cook) 
         Heat 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil in a large Dutch oven or heavy pot over medium heat, then add the diced onion, celery, and carrot and cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, about 7 minutes.
      6. [ T-38] (  1m cook) 
         Add the 3 sliced garlic cloves and ¼ teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional) and cook, stirring, until fragrant, about 1 minute.
      7. [ T-31] (  6m cook) 
         Pour in all 8 cups of broth (with the Parmigiano rind if using), raise the heat to medium-high, and bring to a gentle boil.
      8. [ T-30] ( 10m cook) 
         Gently drop the meatballs one by one into the simmering broth, reduce heat to medium-low, and cook uncovered for 10 minutes until the meatballs are cooked through.
      9. [ T-24] (  8m cook) 
         Stir in ½ cup acini di pepe pasta and cook for 8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the pasta is just tender.
     10. [ T-14] (  3m cook) 
         Add the 1 pound of torn escarole leaves in two or three batches, stirring each batch down into the broth until wilted, about 3 minutes total; remove and discard the Parmigiano rind if used.
     11. [  T-6] (  3m assemble) 
         Taste the soup and adjust seasoning with kosher salt and black pepper, then ladle into bowls and finish each with a generous grating of Parmigiano-Reggiano.

  ▸ Shrimp and Crab Jambalaya   [Monday]  FLATTENED (single-dish)
    footer 66 min | scheduler 66 | card 66 | fresh derive 66 | dishes 1
      1. [ T-66] ( 10m prep) 
         Dice the yellow onion, green bell pepper, and 2 celery stalks; mince the 4 garlic cloves; and slice the 8 oz andouille sausage into ¼-inch rounds.
      2. [ T-56] (  2m prep) 
         In a small bowl, combine 2 teaspoons Cajun seasoning, 1 teaspoon smoked paprika, ½ teaspoon dried thyme, ½ teaspoon dried oregano, and ¼ teaspoon cayenne pepper.
      3. [    —] (  5m prep) 
         Pick over the 8 oz lump crab meat for any shell fragments and thinly slice the 3 green onions and roughly chop the ¼ cup parsley; set aside for garnish.
      4. [ T-54] (  2m preheat) 
         Heat 2 tablespoons olive oil in a large, heavy-bottomed pot or Dutch oven over medium-high heat until shimmering.
      5. [ T-49] (  4m cook) 
         Add the andouille rounds in a single layer and sear 3–4 minutes, turning once, until browned on both sides; transfer to a plate.
      6. [ T-47] (  6m cook) 
         Add the onion, bell pepper, and celery to the pot and sauté over medium heat for 5 minutes, stirring frequently, until softened; add the garlic and cook 1 minute more.
      7. [ T-43] (  3m cook) 
         Stir in the spice blend, 1 teaspoon kosher salt, and ½ teaspoon black pepper, then add the 14 oz crushed tomatoes, 2½ cups chicken broth, 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce, 1 teaspoon hot sauce (if using), and 2 bay leaves; return the sausage to the pot.
      8. [ T-37] ( 23m cook) 
         Stir in 1½ cups long-grain white rice, bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover tightly, and cook for 20 minutes without lifting the lid.
      9. [ T-34] (  6m cook) 
         Nestle the 1 pound shrimp into the rice, re-cover, and cook on low for 5–6 minutes until the shrimp are pink and just cooked through.
     10. [ T-11] (  3m rest) 
         Gently fold in the lump crab meat, remove and discard the 2 bay leaves, and let the pot rest off the heat, covered, for 3 minutes.
     11. [  T-5] (  2m assemble) 
         Serve directly from the pot, garnished with the sliced green onions and chopped parsley.

  ▸ Hyderabadi Dum Chicken Biryani   [Wednesday]  FLATTENED (single-dish)
    footer 101 min | scheduler 101 | card 101 | fresh derive 101 | dishes 1
      1. [T-101] (  2m prep) 
         Steep ¼ teaspoon saffron in 3 tablespoons warm whole milk and set aside to bloom for at least 10 minutes.
      2. [ T-99] (  8m prep) 
         In a large bowl, combine the chicken pieces with ¾ cup yogurt, 1½ tablespoons grated ginger, 6 minced garlic cloves, 3 slit green chilies, 2 tablespoons lemon juice, 2 teaspoons kosher salt, and half the fried onions (½ cup); toss well, cover, and marinate at room temperature while you prepare everything else.
      3. [ T-91] ( 25m cook) 
         Heat ½ cup neutral oil in a wide, heavy-bottomed pot over medium-high; add the 2 thinly sliced yellow onions and fry, stirring frequently, for 20–25 minutes until deep golden and crisp.
      4. [ T-66] (  2m cook) 
         Use a slotted spoon to transfer the fried onions to a paper-towel-lined plate; reserve the onion oil in the pot.
      5. [    —] (  2m cook) 
         In the pot with the reserved onion oil (or ghee), add 5 cardamom pods, 4 cloves, 1 cinnamon stick, 2 bay leaves, ½ teaspoon black peppercorns, and 1 star anise; fry over medium heat for 1–2 minutes until fragrant.
      6. [ T-64] (  1m cook) 
         Add 1½ teaspoons Kashmiri red chili powder, 1 teaspoon ground coriander, ½ teaspoon ground cumin, 1 teaspoon garam masala, and ½ teaspoon turmeric to the pot and stir for 30 seconds.
      7. [ T-62] ( 10m cook) 
         Add the marinated chicken (with all its marinade) to the spiced pot; spread into an even layer and cook over medium-high heat, stirring occasionally, for 8–10 minutes until the chicken is no longer pink on the outside and the marinade has reduced and coats the meat.
      8. [    —] ( 10m cook) 
         While the chicken cooks, bring 6 cups water to a rolling boil in a large separate pot with 1 teaspoon kosher salt; add the drained basmati rice and parboil for exactly 6 minutes (the rice should be about 70% cooked — still firm in the center), then drain immediately.
      9. [ T-61] (  3m assemble) 
         Spread the parboiled rice evenly over the chicken layer in the pot; scatter the remaining ½ cup fried onions, ½ cup torn mint leaves, and ½ cup chopped cilantro over the rice.
     10. [ T-51] (  1m assemble) 
         Drizzle the saffron milk and 2 tablespoons ghee over the top of the rice layer.
     11. [ T-41] ( 25m cook) 
         Cover the pot with a tight-fitting lid (seal the edge with a strip of dough or foil if needed to trap steam), reduce heat to the lowest setting, and cook on dum for 25 minutes — do not lift the lid.
     12. [ T-38] ( 10m rest) 
         Remove from heat and let the biryani rest, lid on, for 10 minutes before opening.
     13. [ T-37] (  2m assemble) 
         Gently fluff and mix the rice and chicken together with a wide spoon or spatula, then serve directly from the pot.

  ▸ Sheet-Pan Shrimp Boil with Sausage, Corn, and Potatoes   [Thursday]  FLATTENED (single-dish)
    footer 68 min | scheduler 49 | card 49 | fresh derive 49 | dishes 1
      1. [ T-49] ( 15m preheat)

      2. [ T-49] (  8m prep)

      3. [ T-41] (  3m prep) 
         In a small bowl, whisk together 2 tablespoons Old Bay seasoning, 1 teaspoon smoked paprika, 1 teaspoon garlic powder, ½ teaspoon onion powder, ¼ teaspoon cayenne pepper, 1 teaspoon kosher salt, and ½ teaspoon black pepper.
      4. [    —] (  3m prep)

      5. [ T-38] ( 20m cook)

      6. [ T-34] (  5m prep)

      7. [ T-34] ( 12m cook) 
         Remove the pan from the oven, nestle the sausage, shrimp, and lemon rounds among the potatoes and corn in a single layer, and roast for 10–12 minutes until the shrimp are pink and just cooked through and the sausage is browned at the edges.

         Scatter 3 tablespoons roughly chopped fresh parsley over the pan and serve directly from the sheet pan with lemon wedges alongside.

         Measure out 2 teaspoons chili powder, 1½ teaspoons ground cumin, 1 teaspoon smoked paprika, ½ teaspoon dried oregano, 1 teaspoon kosher salt, and ¼ teaspoon black pepper into a small bowl and set aside.
      4. [    —] (  3m prep) 
         Drain and rinse the 15 oz can of black beans and roughly chop the ¼ cup fresh cilantro; set both aside.
      5. [ T-62] (  6m cook) 
         Brown the 1¼ lb ground beef in a large skillet over medium-high heat, breaking it up with a spoon, for about 6 minutes until no pink remains; drain excess fat.
      6. [ T-59] (  4m cook) 
         Reduce heat to medium, add the diced onion to the beef, and cook 3 minutes until softened, then stir in the minced garlic and cook 1 minute more.
      7. [ T-53] (  2m cook) 
         Stir the spice blend (chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, oregano, salt, and pepper) and 2 tablespoons tomato paste into the beef, coating everything evenly and cooking 1 minute.
      8. [ T-49] (  2m cook) 
         Fold the drained black beans and 1½ cups chunky salsa into the beef mixture and stir to combine; remove from heat.
      9. [    —] (  2m prep) 
         Toss the 2 cups shredded cheddar and 1 cup Monterey Jack together in a bowl to make the cheese blend.
     10. [ T-47] (  3m assemble) 
         Spread a thin layer of the beef mixture across the bottom of the prepared baking dish, then lay 4 corn tortillas in an overlapping single layer on top.
     11. [ T-47] (  5m assemble) 
         Spoon half the remaining beef mixture over the tortillas and scatter ⅓ of the cheese blend on top; repeat with 4 more tortillas, the rest of the beef, and another ⅓ of the cheese, then finish with the final 4 tortillas and the remaining cheese.
     12. [ T-45] ( 30m cook) 
         Cover the dish tightly with foil and bake at 375 °F for 20 minutes, then uncover and bake a further 10 minutes until the cheese is melted and bubbling at the edges.
     13. [ T-42] (  5m rest) 
         Let the casserole rest uncovered for 5 minutes so the layers set before cutting.
     14. [ T-37] (  2m assemble) 
         Dollop ½ cup sour cream across the top, scatter the chopped cilantro, and add the 2 tablespoons pickled jalapeño slices (optional) before serving straight from the dish.
