    footer 50 min | scheduler 50 | card 50 | fresh derive 50 | dishes 4
      1. [ T-50] (  6m prep) Smoky Chipotle Ground Beef
         Finely dice the yellow onion and mince the 4 garlic cloves; mince 2 chipotle peppers in adobo and measure out 1 tablespoon of adobo sauce.
      2. [ T-44] (  4m prep) Quick Pickled Red Onions
         Thinly slice 1 red onion and pack the slices into a pint jar or heatproof bowl.
      3. [ T-40] (  2m preheat) Smoky Chipotle Ground Beef
         Heat 1 tablespoon neutral oil in a large skillet over medium-high until shimmering.
      4. [ T-40] (  2m cook) Quick Pickled Red Onions
         ⟶ With the Smoky Chipotle Ground Beef heating up, start on the Quick Pickled Red Onions.
         Combine ½ cup apple cider vinegar, 1 teaspoon granulated sugar, and ½ teaspoon kosher salt in a small saucepan and heat over medium, stirring, until the sugar and salt dissolve, about 2 minutes.
      5. [ T-38] (  4m cook) Smoky Chipotle Ground Beef
         Add the diced onion and cook, stirring frequently, for 4 minutes until softened and lightly golden.
      6. [ T-38] ( 20m rest) Quick Pickled Red Onions
         Pour the hot brine over the sliced onions, press them down to submerge, and let sit at room temperature for at least 20 minutes before serving.
      7. [ T-34] (  1m cook) Smoky Chipotle Ground Beef
         ⟶ With the Quick Pickled Red Onions marinating, start on the Smoky Chipotle Ground Beef.
         Add the minced garlic and cook, stirring constantly, for 1 minute until fragrant.
      8. [ T-33] (  4m prep) Avocado Crema
         ⟶ With the Quick Pickled Red Onions marinating, start on the Avocado Crema.
         Halve and pit 2 ripe avocados and scoop the flesh into a blender or food processor; mince 1 garlic clove and roughly chop ¼ cup fresh cilantro.
      9. [ T-29] (  6m cook) Smoky Chipotle Ground Beef
         ⟶ With the Quick Pickled Red Onions marinating, start on the Smoky Chipotle Ground Beef.
         Add 1½ pounds 80/20 ground beef and cook, breaking it up with a spoon, for 6 minutes until browned and no pink remains; drain excess fat.
     10. [ T-23] (  1m cook) Smoky Chipotle Ground Beef
         Stir in the minced chipotle peppers, 1 tablespoon adobo sauce, 2 tablespoons tomato paste, 1½ teaspoons ground cumin, 1 teaspoon smoked paprika, ½ teaspoon dried oregano, 1 teaspoon kosher salt, and ½ teaspoon black pepper; cook 1 minute.
     11. [ T-22] (  5m cook) Smoky Chipotle Ground Beef
         Pour in ¼ cup beef broth, stir to combine, and simmer over medium-low for 5 minutes until the liquid is mostly absorbed and the beef is saucy.
     12. [ T-22] (  5m prep) Warm Corn Tortillas & Garnishes
         ⟶ With the Quick Pickled Red Onions marinating, start on the Warm Corn Tortillas & Garnishes.
         Cut 2 limes into wedges, pick ½ bunch fresh cilantro into sprigs, and, if using, thinly slice 1 jalapeño and crumble 3 ounces cotija cheese.
     13. [ T-17] (  2m hold) Smoky Chipotle Ground Beef
         Taste and adjust salt, then remove from heat and keep warm until ready to build tacos.
     14. [ T-17] (  2m assemble) Avocado Crema
         ⟶ With the Smoky Chipotle Ground Beef staying warm, start on the Avocado Crema.
         Add ½ cup sour cream, 2 tablespoons fresh lime juice, the minced garlic, ½ teaspoon kosher salt, and the chopped cilantro to the blender and blend until completely smooth.
     15. [ T-15] (  2m assemble) Avocado Crema
         Taste and adjust lime juice or salt, then transfer to a serving bowl and press plastic wrap directly onto the surface until ready to serve.
     16. [ T-13] (  8m cook) Warm Corn Tortillas & Garnishes
         Warm 16 corn tortillas in a dry skillet over medium heat for 30 seconds per side, stacking them in a clean kitchen towel to keep warm, working in batches.
     17. [  T-5] (  2m assemble) Smoky Chipotle Ground Beef
         Spoon the chipotle beef into warm tortillas and serve immediately.
     18. [  T-3] (  2m assemble) Warm Corn Tortillas & Garnishes
         Arrange the warm tortillas, cilantro sprigs, lime wedges, pickled onions, avocado crema, and, if using, cotija cheese and jalapeño slices on the table for guests to build their own tacos.
     19. [  T-1] (  1m assemble) Quick Pickled Red Onions
         Drain lightly and transfer to a small serving dish alongside the tacos.
