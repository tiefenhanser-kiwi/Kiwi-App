  ▸ Classic Tex-Mex Sheet-Pan Chicken Fajitas   [Monday]  SEQUENCED
    footer 46 min | scheduler 46 | card 46 | fresh derive 46 | dishes 2
      1. [ T-46] ( 12m preheat) Sheet-Pan Chicken Fajitas
         Preheat the oven to 425°F and position a rack in the upper third.
      2. [ T-46] (  3m prep) Sheet-Pan Chicken Fajitas
         In a large bowl, whisk together 3 tablespoons olive oil, 2 teaspoons chili powder, 1 teaspoon cumin, 1 teaspoon smoked paprika, ½ teaspoon garlic powder, ½ teaspoon onion powder, ½ teaspoon dried oregano, 1 teaspoon kosher salt, and ½ teaspoon black pepper.
      3. [ T-43] (  8m prep) Sheet-Pan Chicken Fajitas
         Cut the chicken thighs into ½-inch strips, thinly slice 2 red bell peppers, 1 green bell pepper, and 1 yellow onion, then add everything to the bowl and toss well to coat.
      4. [ T-34] (  2m cook) Sheet-Pan Chicken Fajitas
         Spread the chicken and vegetables in a single layer across one or two large sheet pans, making sure not to crowd them.
      5. [ T-32] ( 22m cook) Sheet-Pan Chicken Fajitas
         Roast at 425°F for 20–22 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through and the edges of the peppers and onions are lightly charred.
      6. [ T-32] (  5m cook) Sheet-Pan Chicken Fajitas
         While the fajita filling roasts, wrap the 8 flour tortillas in foil and place them in the oven for the last 5 minutes to warm through.
      7. [ T-10] (  2m assemble) Sheet-Pan Chicken Fajitas
         Remove the sheet pan from the oven, squeeze the juice of 1 lime over the chicken and vegetables, and toss to combine.
      8. [  T-8] (  4m prep) Fajita Toppings
         Roughly chop ¼ bunch of fresh cilantro and thinly slice 1 jalapeño (optional), then arrange in small serving bowls.
      9. [  T-4] (  2m assemble) Sheet-Pan Chicken Fajitas
         Transfer the fajita filling to a serving platter and bring to the table with the warm tortillas and toppings.
     10. [  T-2] (  2m assemble) Fajita Toppings
         Cut 1 lime into wedges and set out alongside ½ cup sour cream, ¾ cup salsa, and 1 cup shredded Mexican cheese blend so all toppings are ready to serve.

    footer 67 min | scheduler 67 | card 67 | fresh derive 67 | dishes 5
      1. [ T-67] (  5m prep) Carne Asada
         In a bowl, whisk together ¼ cup orange juice, 3 tablespoons lime juice, 4 minced garlic cloves, 2 tablespoons olive oil, 1 teaspoon cumin, 1 teaspoon chili powder, ½ teaspoon smoked paprika, ½ teaspoon dried oregano, 1 teaspoon kosher salt, and ½ teaspoon black pepper.
      2. [ T-62] ( 30m rest) Carne Asada
         Place 1½ pounds skirt steak in a zip-top bag or shallow dish, pour the marinade over it, seal, and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes (up to 4 hours).
