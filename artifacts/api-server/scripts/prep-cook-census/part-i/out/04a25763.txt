  ▸ Texas-Style Smoked Brisket with Pinto Beans and Pickled Jalapeños   [Wednesday]  SEQUENCED
    footer 226 min | scheduler 226 | card 226 | fresh derive 226 | dishes 3
      1. [T-226] (  3m prep) Texas-Style Smoked Brisket
         Pat the 2½-pound brisket flat dry with paper towels, then rub 1 tablespoon neutral oil evenly over all sides.
      2. [T-223] (  3m prep) Texas-Style Smoked Brisket
         Mix 2 tablespoons coarse kosher salt, 2 tablespoons coarsely ground black pepper, 1 teaspoon garlic powder, and 1 teaspoon smoked paprika in a small bowl.
      3. [T-220] (  3m prep) Texas-Style Smoked Brisket
         Press the rub firmly onto all sides of the oiled brisket, coating evenly.
      4. [T-217] ( 15m preheat) Texas-Style Smoked Brisket
         Preheat the oven to 300°F.
      5. [T-217] (  2m prep) Texas-Style Smoked Brisket
         Whisk together ½ cup beef broth, 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce, and 1½ teaspoons liquid smoke in a small bowl.
      6. [T-215] (  8m cook) Texas-Style Smoked Brisket
         Heat a large oven-safe Dutch oven or heavy skillet over high heat until smoking; sear the brisket fat-side down for 4 minutes, then flip and sear the other side for 3 minutes until a deep brown crust forms.
      7. [T-202] (180m cook) Texas-Style Smoked Brisket
         Pour the broth mixture into the bottom of the Dutch oven around (not over) the brisket, cover tightly with a lid or heavy-duty foil, and braise in the oven for 3 hours until fork-tender.
      8. [T-139] (  4m prep) Slow-Cooked Pinto Beans
         ⟶ With the Texas-Style Smoked Brisket cooking, start on the Slow-Cooked Pinto Beans.
         Roughly chop the yellow onion and smash 4 garlic cloves.
      9. [T-135] (  2m prep) Slow-Cooked Pinto Beans
         Drain and rinse the overnight-soaked 1½ cups dried pinto beans.
     10. [T-133] (  3m cook) Slow-Cooked Pinto Beans
         Combine the drained dried beans, onion, garlic, smoked ham hock, 3 cups beef broth, 1 cup water, ½ teaspoon dried oregano, ½ teaspoon ground cumin, 1 teaspoon kosher salt, ½ teaspoon black pepper, and 1 bay leaf in a large pot.
     11. [T-130] (120m cook) Slow-Cooked Pinto Beans
         Bring the pot to a boil over high heat, then reduce to a gentle simmer, cover partially, and cook for 2 hours until the dried beans are completely tender and creamy, adding water ¼ cup at a time if the liquid drops below the beans.
     12. [ T-34] (  5m prep) Pickled Jalapeños
         ⟶ With the Texas-Style Smoked Brisket cooking, start on the Pickled Jalapeños.
         Slice 6 fresh jalapeños into ¼-inch rounds and thinly slice 2 garlic cloves.
     13. [ T-29] (  5m cook) Pickled Jalapeños
         Combine ½ cup white distilled vinegar, ½ cup water, 1 teaspoon granulated sugar, 1 teaspoon kosher salt, and ½ teaspoon black peppercorns in a small saucepan and bring to a boil over medium-high heat, stirring until the salt and sugar dissolve.
     14. [ T-24] ( 20m rest) Pickled Jalapeños
         Pack the jalapeño rounds and sliced garlic into a heatproof jar or bowl, pour the hot brine over them, and let cool to room temperature for at least 20 minutes before serving.
     15. [ T-22] ( 15m rest) Texas-Style Smoked Brisket
         ⟶ With the Slow-Cooked Pinto Beans marinating, start on the Texas-Style Smoked Brisket.
         Remove the brisket from the oven, tent loosely with foil, and rest for 15 minutes before slicing.
     16. [ T-10] (  5m cook) Slow-Cooked Pinto Beans
         ⟶ With the Pickled Jalapeños marinating, start on the Slow-Cooked Pinto Beans.
         Remove the ham hock and bay leaf; pull any meat from the hock, shred it, and stir it back into the beans.
     17. [  T-7] (  4m assemble) Texas-Style Smoked Brisket
         ⟶ With the Pickled Jalapeños marinating, start on the Texas-Style Smoked Brisket.
         Slice the brisket against the grain into ½-inch-thick slabs and arrange on a serving platter; spoon any pan juices over the top.
     18. [  T-3] (  2m assemble) Slow-Cooked Pinto Beans
         Taste and adjust salt, then ladle the beans into a serving bowl.
     19. [  T-1] (  1m assemble) Pickled Jalapeños
         Set the jalapeños on the table alongside the brisket and beans for guests to add as desired.

     13. [ T-18] (  2m cook) Creamy White Bean Mash
         Heat 3 tablespoons olive oil and 2 tablespoons butter in a medium saucepan over medium; add the minced garlic and 1 teaspoon finely minced fresh rosemary and cook, stirring, for 90 seconds until fragrant but not browned.
     14. [ T-15] (  4m cook) Creamy White Bean Mash

     15. [ T-14] (  6m prep) Lemon-Parsley Gremolata
         ⟶ With the Red Wine Braised Lamb Shanks cooking, start on the Lemon-Parsley Gremolata.

     17. [  T-8] (  3m cook) Creamy White Bean Mash

     18. [  T-8] (  2m assemble) Lemon-Parsley Gremolata

         ⟶ With the Red Wine Braised Lamb Shanks resting, start on the Creamy White Bean Mash.

      1. [ T-38] (  3m prep) Cilantro-Lime Rice
         Rinse 1½ cups long-grain white rice in a fine-mesh strainer under cold water, stirring with your hand, until the water runs clear.
      2. [ T-35] (  5m cook) Cilantro-Lime Rice
         Combine the rinsed rice, 2¼ cups water, ½ teaspoon kosher salt, and 1 tablespoon unsalted butter in a medium saucepan and bring to a boil over medium-high heat.
      3. [ T-30] (  5m prep) Mango Avocado Salsa
         Peel and dice 1 ripe mango into ½-inch cubes and finely dice ¼ of a red onion; add both to a medium bowl.
      4. [ T-25] (  3m prep) Blackened Salmon
         In a small bowl, combine 1½ teaspoons smoked paprika, 1 teaspoon garlic powder, ½ teaspoon onion powder, ½ teaspoon dried oregano, ½ teaspoon dried thyme, ¼ teaspoon cayenne, ½ teaspoon black pepper, and ¾ teaspoon kosher salt; stir together into a blackening rub.
      5. [ T-25] ( 15m cook) Cilantro-Lime Rice
         Reduce the heat to the lowest setting, cover tightly, and cook for 15 minutes until all water is absorbed and the rice is tender.
      6. [ T-22] (  4m prep) Mango Avocado Salsa
         ⟶ With the Cilantro-Lime Rice cooking, start on the Mango Avocado Salsa.
         Seed and finely mince 1 jalapeño, then roughly chop 3 tablespoons fresh cilantro; add both to the bowl.
      7. [ T-18] (  3m prep) Blackened Salmon
         ⟶ With the Cilantro-Lime Rice cooking, start on the Blackened Salmon.
         Pat the four 6-oz skin-on salmon fillets thoroughly dry with paper towels, then press the blackening rub firmly onto all flesh-side surfaces of each fillet.
      8. [ T-15] (  2m prep) Mango Avocado Salsa
         ⟶ With the Cilantro-Lime Rice cooking, start on the Mango Avocado Salsa.
         Squeeze 2 tablespoons fresh lime juice over the bowl, add ¼ teaspoon kosher salt, and stir gently to combine.
      9. [ T-13] (  3m preheat) Blackened Salmon
         ⟶ With the Cilantro-Lime Rice cooking, start on the Blackened Salmon.
         Heat 2 tablespoons neutral oil in a large cast-iron or heavy skillet over medium-high until the oil just begins to smoke.
     10. [ T-13] (  4m assemble) Mango Avocado Salsa
         ⟶ With the Cilantro-Lime Rice cooking, start on the Mango Avocado Salsa.
         Just before serving, pit, peel, and dice 2 ripe avocados into ½-inch cubes and fold them gently into the salsa so they stay intact and don't brown.
     11. [ T-10] (  5m rest) Cilantro-Lime Rice
         Remove the pan from the heat and let the rice steam, covered and undisturbed, for 5 minutes.
     12. [  T-9] (  4m cook) Blackened Salmon
         ⟶ With the Cilantro-Lime Rice cooking, start on the Blackened Salmon.
         Place the fillets skin-side up in the skillet and cook undisturbed for 3–4 minutes until a dark, charred crust forms on the flesh side.
     13. [  T-5] (  3m cook) Blackened Salmon
         Flip the fillets skin-side down and cook a further 2–3 minutes until the skin is crisp and the fish is just cooked through and flakes easily at the thickest point.
     14. [  T-2] (  2m rest) Blackened Salmon
         Transfer the salmon to a plate and rest 2 minutes, then serve with lime wedges alongside.
     15. [  T-2] (  2m assemble) Cilantro-Lime Rice
         ⟶ With the Blackened Salmon resting, start on the Cilantro-Lime Rice.
         Uncover, drizzle in 2 tablespoons fresh lime juice, add ½ teaspoon lime zest and ¼ cup finely chopped fresh cilantro, then fluff and fold everything together with a fork.
