         ⟶ With the Seasoned Sushi Rice marinating, start on the Shoyu Marinated Ahi Tuna Poke.

     10. [ T-21] ( 10m rest) Seasoned Sushi Rice

     11. [ T-15] (  2m prep) Sriracha Mayo Drizzle
         ⟶ With the Shoyu Marinated Ahi Tuna Poke chilling, start on the Sriracha Mayo Drizzle.

         Whisk together the Kewpie mayonnaise, sriracha, and lime juice in a small bowl until smooth and fully combined.

         Divide the seasoned sushi rice evenly among 4 bowls, spreading it in an even layer as the base for the poke toppings.
     16. [  T-1] (  1m assemble) Sriracha Mayo Drizzle
         Transfer the sriracha mayo to a small squeeze bottle or spoon it into a zip-lock bag with one corner snipped for easy drizzling over the assembled poke bowls.
