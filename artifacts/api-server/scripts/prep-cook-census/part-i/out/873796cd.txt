      3. [T-18] (cook) Beer-Battered Cod Fish Tacos
         Warm the 12 corn tortillas directly over a gas flame or in a dry skillet for 30 seconds per side until pliable and lightly charred, then stack in a clean towel to keep warm.
      4. [T-12] (assemble) Beer-Battered Cod Fish Tacos
         Set out the fried cod, warm tortillas, ½ cup cilantro leaves, ¼ cup pickled jalapeño slices (optional), and lime wedges from 2 limes so everyone can build their own tacos.
      5. [T-12] (rest) Quick Cabbage Slaw and Lime Crema
         Cover and refrigerate the slaw for at least 10 minutes to allow the cabbage to soften slightly and the flavors to meld.
      6. [T-2] (assemble) Quick Cabbage Slaw and Lime Crema
         Give the slaw a final toss, taste for seasoning, and transfer to a serving bowl alongside the lime crema — spoon directly onto tacos at the table.
