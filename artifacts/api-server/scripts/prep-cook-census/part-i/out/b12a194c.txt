  ▸ Smoky Salisbury Steak with Roasted Garlic Gravy and Green Beans   [Sunday]  SEQUENCED
    footer 67 min | scheduler 67 | card 67 | fresh derive 67 | dishes 3
      1. [ T-67] ( 40m cook) Smoky Salisbury Steak with Roasted Garlic Gravy
         Trim the top ¼ inch off the whole garlic head to expose the cloves, drizzle with 1 teaspoon olive oil, wrap tightly in foil, and roast at 400°F for 40 minutes until the cloves are golden and completely soft.
      2. [ T-67] (  6m prep) Smoky Salisbury Steak with Roasted Garlic Gravy
         While the garlic roasts, combine 1½ lb ground beef, ⅓ cup breadcrumbs, 3 tablespoons whole milk, 1 egg, 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce, 1½ teaspoons smoked paprika, ½ teaspoon garlic powder, ½ teaspoon onion powder, ¾ teaspoon kosher salt, and ½ teaspoon black pepper in a large bowl and mix until just combined.
      3. [ T-61] (  3m prep) Smoky Salisbury Steak with Roasted Garlic Gravy
         Divide the mixture into 4 equal oval patties, about ¾ inch thick, and set aside on a plate.
      4. [ T-58] (  5m prep) Smoky Salisbury Steak with Roasted Garlic Gravy
         Thinly slice the yellow onion and thinly slice the 8 oz cremini mushrooms.
      5. [ T-53] (  9m cook) Smoky Salisbury Steak with Roasted Garlic Gravy
         Heat 1 tablespoon neutral oil in a large skillet over medium-high and sear the patties 4 minutes per side until a deep brown crust forms; transfer to a plate and tent with foil.
      6. [ T-44] (  7m prep) Creamy Mashed Potatoes
         ⟶ With the Smoky Salisbury Steak with Roasted Garlic Gravy cooking, start on the Creamy Mashed Potatoes.
         Peel and cut 2 lb Yukon Gold potatoes into 1½-inch chunks.
      7. [ T-37] (  7m cook) Smoky Salisbury Steak with Roasted Garlic Gravy
         Reduce heat to medium, add 2 tablespoons butter to the same skillet, and sauté the sliced onion and mushrooms for 7 minutes, stirring often, until softened and golden.
      8. [ T-30] ( 22m cook) Creamy Mashed Potatoes
         ⟶ With the Smoky Salisbury Steak with Roasted Garlic Gravy cooking, start on the Creamy Mashed Potatoes.
         Place the potato chunks in a large pot, cover with cold salted water by 1 inch, bring to a boil, then reduce to a steady simmer and cook for 18 minutes until a fork slides in with no resistance.
      9. [ T-30] (  2m cook) Creamy Mashed Potatoes
         Warm ½ cup whole milk in a small saucepan or microwave until steaming.
     10. [ T-27] (  2m cook) Smoky Salisbury Steak with Roasted Garlic Gravy
         ⟶ With the Creamy Mashed Potatoes marinating, start on the Smoky Salisbury Steak with Roasted Garlic Gravy.
         Squeeze the roasted garlic cloves out of their skins into the skillet, sprinkle in 2 tablespoons flour, and stir constantly for 1 minute to cook out the raw flour taste.
     11. [ T-25] (  5m prep) Garlic-Buttered Green Beans
         ⟶ With the Creamy Mashed Potatoes marinating, start on the Garlic-Buttered Green Beans.
         Trim the stem ends from 1 lb fresh green beans and mince 2 garlic cloves.
     12. [ T-20] (  3m cook) Smoky Salisbury Steak with Roasted Garlic Gravy
         ⟶ With the Creamy Mashed Potatoes marinating, start on the Smoky Salisbury Steak with Roasted Garlic Gravy.
         Pour in 2 cups beef broth while scraping up any browned bits, then stir in 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce, ½ teaspoon smoked paprika, and 3 sprigs fresh thyme; season with ¼ teaspoon kosher salt and ¼ teaspoon black pepper.
     13. [ T-20] (  7m cook) Garlic-Buttered Green Beans
         ⟶ With the Creamy Mashed Potatoes marinating, start on the Garlic-Buttered Green Beans.
         Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil, add the green beans, and cook for 4 minutes until crisp-tender.
     14. [ T-17] ( 10m cook) Smoky Salisbury Steak with Roasted Garlic Gravy
         ⟶ With the Creamy Mashed Potatoes marinating, start on the Smoky Salisbury Steak with Roasted Garlic Gravy.
         Nestle the seared patties back into the gravy, cover, and simmer over medium-low for 10 minutes until the patties are cooked through and the gravy has thickened; remove thyme sprigs if using.
     15. [ T-13] (  2m rest) Garlic-Buttered Green Beans
         ⟶ With the Creamy Mashed Potatoes marinating, start on the Garlic-Buttered Green Beans.
         Drain the beans and immediately transfer to a bowl of ice water for 2 minutes to stop cooking, then drain again.
     16. [ T-13] (  2m cook) Garlic-Buttered Green Beans
         Melt 1½ tablespoons butter in a large skillet over medium heat, add the minced garlic, and sauté for 1 minute until fragrant but not browned.
     17. [ T-11] (  2m cook) Garlic-Buttered Green Beans
         Add the drained green beans to the skillet, season with ½ teaspoon kosher salt and ¼ teaspoon black pepper, and toss to coat for 2 minutes until heated through; add ¼ teaspoon red pepper flakes if using.
     18. [  T-9] (  1m assemble) Garlic-Buttered Green Beans
         Remove from heat, drizzle with 1 teaspoon lemon juice, toss once more, and serve.
     19. [  T-8] (  2m cook) Creamy Mashed Potatoes
         ⟶ With the Smoky Salisbury Steak with Roasted Garlic Gravy cooking, start on the Creamy Mashed Potatoes.
         Drain the potatoes thoroughly, return them to the pot over low heat for 1 minute to steam off excess moisture, then remove from heat.
     20. [  T-6] (  4m assemble) Creamy Mashed Potatoes
         Mash the potatoes with 3 tablespoons butter until the butter is fully melted and absorbed, then stir in the warmed milk, 1 teaspoon kosher salt, ¼ teaspoon black pepper, and 2 tablespoons sour cream if using, until smooth and creamy.
     21. [  T-2] (  2m assemble) Smoky Salisbury Steak with Roasted Garlic Gravy
         Spoon the gravy over the patties and serve immediately.

      1. [ T-91] (  3m prep) Spatchcocked Roast Chicken with Smoked Paprika
         Pat the 4-pound spatchcocked chicken completely dry with paper towels and set it breast-side up on a rimmed baking sheet fitted with a wire rack.
      2. [ T-88] (  3m prep) Spatchcocked Roast Chicken with Smoked Paprika
         In a small bowl, combine 2 teaspoons smoked paprika, 1 teaspoon garlic powder, ½ teaspoon onion powder, ½ teaspoon dried thyme, ½ teaspoon dried oregano, 1½ teaspoons kosher salt, and ¾ teaspoon black pepper.
      3. [ T-85] (  3m prep) Spatchcocked Roast Chicken with Smoked Paprika
         Mix 2 tablespoons olive oil, 1 tablespoon softened unsalted butter, and 4 minced garlic cloves into the spice rub to form a paste.
      4. [ T-82] (  4m prep) Spatchcocked Roast Chicken with Smoked Paprika
         Rub the paste all over the chicken — under the breast skin, over the thighs and drumsticks, and across the backbone side.
      5. [ T-78] ( 15m preheat) Spatchcocked Roast Chicken with Smoked Paprika
         Preheat the oven to 425°F and let the rubbed chicken sit at room temperature while the oven heats, about 15 minutes.
      6. [ T-63] ( 43m cook) Spatchcocked Roast Chicken with Smoked Paprika
         Roast the chicken breast-side up for 40–45 minutes, until the breast registers 160°F and the thigh registers 175°F on an instant-read thermometer.

         ⟶ With the Spatchcocked Roast Chicken with Smoked Paprika cooking, start on the Crispy Roasted Potatoes.
         Cut 1½ pounds Yukon Gold potatoes into 1-inch chunks and toss them in a large bowl with 2 tablespoons olive oil, ½ teaspoon smoked paprika, ½ teaspoon garlic powder, ¾ teaspoon kosher salt, and ¼ teaspoon black pepper until evenly coated.
      8. [ T-48] (  2m prep) Crispy Roasted Potatoes
         Roughly chop 2 tablespoons fresh flat-leaf parsley and set aside for garnish.
      9. [ T-46] ( 38m cook) Crispy Roasted Potatoes
         Spread the potatoes in a single layer on a rimmed baking sheet (use a second sheet if needed to avoid crowding) and roast at 425°F for 35–40 minutes, flipping once halfway through, until deeply golden and crispy on the edges.
     10. [ T-22] (  5m prep) Garlic Sautéed Green Beans
         ⟶ With the Spatchcocked Roast Chicken with Smoked Paprika cooking, start on the Garlic Sautéed Green Beans.
         Trim 1 pound fresh green beans and thinly slice 3 garlic cloves.
     11. [ T-20] ( 10m rest) Spatchcocked Roast Chicken with Smoked Paprika
         ⟶ With the Crispy Roasted Potatoes cooking, start on the Spatchcocked Roast Chicken with Smoked Paprika.
         Rest the chicken on the rack, uncovered, for 10 minutes to let the juices redistribute.
     12. [ T-17] (  2m preheat) Garlic Sautéed Green Beans
         ⟶ With the Crispy Roasted Potatoes cooking, start on the Garlic Sautéed Green Beans.
         Heat 1 tablespoon olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high until shimmering.
     13. [ T-15] (  5m cook) Garlic Sautéed Green Beans
         Add the green beans and cook, tossing occasionally, for 4–5 minutes until bright green and beginning to blister.
     14. [ T-10] (  2m cook) Garlic Sautéed Green Beans
         Push the beans to the side, add the sliced garlic to the center of the pan, and cook 60 seconds until fragrant and just golden, then toss everything together.
     15. [  T-8] (  2m assemble) Crispy Roasted Potatoes
         Transfer the roasted potatoes to a serving bowl, scatter the chopped parsley over the top, and serve hot.
     16. [  T-6] (  4m assemble) Spatchcocked Roast Chicken with Smoked Paprika
         Squeeze the halved lemon over the chicken, carve into pieces, and serve immediately.
     17. [  T-2] (  2m assemble) Garlic Sautéed Green Beans
         Season with ½ teaspoon kosher salt, ¼ teaspoon black pepper, and ¼ teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional), then remove from heat and squeeze 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice over the beans.
