  ▸ Classic Chicken Tikka Masala with Basmati Rice and Garlic Naan   [Sunday]  SEQUENCED
    footer 55 min | scheduler 55 | card 55 | fresh derive 55 | dishes 3
      1. [ T-55] (  3m prep) Chicken Tikka Masala
         In a large bowl, combine ½ cup whole-milk yogurt, 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice, 1 teaspoon kosher salt, and the marinade spices.
      2. [ T-52] (  2m prep) Chicken Tikka Masala
         Measure ½ teaspoon cumin, ½ teaspoon coriander, 1 teaspoon garam masala, ½ teaspoon smoked paprika, ¼ teaspoon turmeric, and ¼ teaspoon cayenne into the yogurt mixture and stir to combine.
      3. [ T-50] ( 10m rest) Chicken Tikka Masala
         Add the 2 pounds of chicken thigh pieces to the marinade, toss to coat thoroughly, and let sit at room temperature while you prepare the sauce (at least 10 minutes).
      4. [ T-50] (  6m prep) Chicken Tikka Masala
         Finely dice the yellow onion, mince the 5 garlic cloves, and finely grate 1 tablespoon fresh ginger.
      5. [ T-40] (  8m cook) Chicken Tikka Masala
         Heat 2 tablespoons neutral oil in a large heavy skillet or Dutch oven over high heat until shimmering, then cook the marinated chicken pieces in a single layer for 4–5 minutes, turning once, until charred in spots and just cooked through; transfer to a plate.
      6. [ T-32] (  6m cook) Chicken Tikka Masala
         Reduce heat to medium, add 2 tablespoons butter to the same pan, and sauté the diced onion for 6 minutes until softened and golden.
      7. [ T-26] (  2m prep) Steamed Basmati Rice
         Rinse 1½ cups basmati rice in a fine-mesh strainer under cold water, swishing until the water runs clear, about 30 seconds.
      8. [ T-24] (  3m cook) Chicken Tikka Masala
         Add the minced garlic and grated ginger and cook 1 minute until fragrant, then stir in 2 tablespoons tomato paste and cook a further 2 minutes until the paste darkens slightly.
      9. [ T-24] (  4m cook) Steamed Basmati Rice
         Combine the rinsed rice, 2¼ cups water, ½ teaspoon kosher salt, and 1 tablespoon unsalted butter in a medium saucepan and bring to a boil over high heat.
     10. [ T-21] (  1m cook) Chicken Tikka Masala
         ⟶ With the Steamed Basmati Rice marinating, start on the Chicken Tikka Masala.
         Stir in 1 teaspoon cumin, ½ teaspoon coriander, 1 teaspoon garam masala, ½ teaspoon smoked paprika, ¼ teaspoon turmeric, and ¼ teaspoon cayenne and cook 30 seconds until fragrant.
     11. [ T-20] ( 10m cook) Chicken Tikka Masala
         Pour in the 14 oz crushed San Marzano tomatoes, stir well, and simmer over medium-low heat for 10 minutes until the sauce thickens and the oil begins to separate at the edges.
     12. [ T-20] ( 15m cook) Steamed Basmati Rice
         ⟶ With the Chicken Tikka Masala cooking, start on the Steamed Basmati Rice.
         Once boiling, stir once, reduce heat to the lowest setting, cover tightly, and cook for 15 minutes without lifting the lid.
     13. [ T-16] (  3m prep) Garlic Naan
         ⟶ With the Chicken Tikka Masala cooking, start on the Garlic Naan.
         Melt 2 tablespoons unsalted butter and stir in 2 minced garlic cloves and 2 tablespoons finely chopped fresh cilantro to make the garlic butter.
     14. [ T-13] (  6m cook) Garlic Naan
         Heat a large dry skillet or grill pan over medium-high heat and warm each of the 4 naan breads for 1–2 minutes per side until lightly toasted and pliable.
     15. [  T-7] (  5m cook) Chicken Tikka Masala
         ⟶ With the Steamed Basmati Rice cooking, start on the Chicken Tikka Masala.
         Stir in ½ cup heavy cream, return the cooked chicken and any resting juices to the pan, and simmer gently for 5 minutes until the chicken is warmed through and the sauce coats it richly.
     16. [  T-7] (  2m assemble) Garlic Naan
         ⟶ With the Steamed Basmati Rice cooking, start on the Garlic Naan.
         Immediately brush each warm naan with the garlic-cilantro butter and sprinkle with ¼ teaspoon flaky sea salt; serve at once.
     17. [  T-5] (  5m rest) Steamed Basmati Rice
         ⟶ With the Chicken Tikka Masala resting, start on the Steamed Basmati Rice.
         Remove from heat and let the rice steam, still covered, for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork and serve.
     18. [  T-2] (  2m assemble) Chicken Tikka Masala
         ⟶ With the Steamed Basmati Rice cooking, start on the Chicken Tikka Masala.
         Taste and adjust salt with the remaining ¼ teaspoon kosher salt if needed, then ladle into bowls and top with ¼ cup roughly chopped fresh cilantro.

  ▸ Chana Masala with Naan and Cucumber Raita   [Wednesday]  SEQUENCED
    footer 54 min | scheduler 54 | card 54 | fresh derive 54 | dishes 3
      1. [ T-54] ( 10m prep) Chana Masala
         Finely dice the 2 yellow onions, mince the 6 garlic cloves, grate 2 tablespoons fresh ginger, and finely chop the 2 serrano chiles (remove seeds for less heat).
      2. [ T-44] (  2m prep) Chana Masala
         Measure out 2 teaspoons ground cumin, 2 teaspoons ground coriander, 1½ teaspoons garam masala, ½ teaspoon turmeric, 1 teaspoon chili powder, and 1 teaspoon amchur into a small bowl and set aside.
      3. [ T-42] (  2m prep) Chana Masala
         Drain and rinse the three 20 oz cans of chickpeas and set aside.
      4. [ T-40] (  9m cook) Chana Masala
         Heat 3 tablespoons neutral oil in a large, deep skillet or Dutch oven over medium-high heat until shimmering, then add the onions and cook, stirring frequently, for 8 minutes until deep golden.
      5. [ T-31] (  2m cook) Chana Masala
         Add the garlic, ginger, and serranos and cook, stirring constantly, for 2 minutes until fragrant.
      6. [ T-29] (  1m cook) Chana Masala
         Add the spice blend and 1½ teaspoons kosher salt to the pan and stir for 1 minute to toast the spices directly into the oil.
      7. [ T-28] (  8m cook) Chana Masala
         Pour in the 28 oz crushed tomatoes and ½ cup water, stir well, and simmer over medium heat for 8 minutes until the sauce thickens and the oil begins to separate at the edges.
      8. [ T-20] ( 10m cook) Chana Masala
         Add the drained chickpeas, stir to coat in the sauce, and simmer over medium-low heat for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the chickpeas are heated through and the curry is thick.
      9. [ T-20] (  6m prep) Cucumber Raita
         ⟶ With the Chana Masala cooking, start on the Cucumber Raita.
         Halve the English cucumber lengthwise, scrape out the seeds with a spoon, and finely dice the flesh; finely chop 2 tablespoons fresh mint leaves and 2 tablespoons fresh cilantro.
     10. [ T-14] (  2m prep) Cucumber Raita
         In a medium bowl, whisk together 2 cups plain whole-milk yogurt, 1 tablespoon lemon juice, ¼ teaspoon ground cumin, and ½ teaspoon kosher salt until smooth.
     11. [ T-12] (  2m assemble) Cucumber Raita
         Fold in the diced cucumber, mint, and cilantro, then cover and refrigerate until ready to serve.
     12. [ T-10] (  2m assemble) Chana Masala
         Remove from heat, squeeze in the juice of 1 lemon, stir, and taste for salt; ladle into bowls and scatter ¼ cup roughly chopped fresh cilantro over the top.
     13. [  T-8] (  6m cook) Store Naan
         Heat a dry skillet or cast-iron pan over medium-high heat until very hot, then warm each of the 8 naan pieces for 30–60 seconds per side until lightly charred and pliable (or hold them directly over a gas flame for the same time).
     14. [  T-2] (  2m assemble) Store Naan
         Brush each warm naan with a little of the 2 tablespoons unsalted butter (optional) and stack under a clean kitchen towel to keep warm until serving.

      1. [ T-84] (  4m prep) Butter Chicken (Murgh Makhani)
         In a large bowl, combine ½ cup plain whole-milk yogurt, 1 tablespoon lemon juice, 1 teaspoon fine sea salt, and the marinade spices (1½ teaspoons garam masala, 1 teaspoon each ground cumin and ground coriander, 1 teaspoon kashmiri chili powder, and ½ teaspoon ground turmeric); whisk until smooth.
      2. [ T-80] ( 15m rest) Butter Chicken (Murgh Makhani)
         Add 2 pounds of chicken thigh pieces to the marinade, toss to coat thoroughly, and set aside while you prepare the sauce (at least 15 minutes, or cover and refrigerate up to 8 hours).
      3. [ T-80] (  6m prep) Butter Chicken (Murgh Makhani)
         Finely dice 1 yellow onion, mince 5 garlic cloves, and grate 1 tablespoon fresh ginger.
      4. [ T-70] (  3m prep) Whole Wheat Roti
         ⟶ With the Butter Chicken (Murgh Makhani) chilling, start on the Whole Wheat Roti.
         In a large bowl, whisk together 2 cups whole wheat flour (atta) and ½ teaspoon fine sea salt, then drizzle in 1 tablespoon neutral oil and rub it into the flour until it resembles coarse crumbs.
      5. [ T-67] ( 25m rest) Whole Wheat Roti
         Add ¾ cup warm water gradually, mixing with your hand, until a soft, pliable dough forms; knead on a lightly floured surface for 5 minutes until smooth, then cover with a damp cloth and rest for 20 minutes.
      6. [ T-65] ( 12m cook) Butter Chicken (Murgh Makhani)
         ⟶ With the Whole Wheat Roti resting, start on the Butter Chicken (Murgh Makhani).
         Heat 2 tablespoons neutral oil in a large heavy-bottomed skillet or Dutch oven over medium-high; cook the marinated chicken in a single layer (in batches if needed) for 4–5 minutes per side until charred at the edges and just cooked through, then transfer to a plate.
      7. [ T-53] (  7m cook) Butter Chicken (Murgh Makhani)
         Reduce heat to medium, add 3 tablespoons unsalted butter to the same pan, and sauté the diced onion for 6–7 minutes until soft and golden, stirring frequently.
      8. [ T-46] ( 12m cook) Butter Chicken (Murgh Makhani)
         Add the minced garlic and grated ginger and cook for 1 minute until fragrant, then pour in the 14 oz can of crushed tomatoes, add 1 teaspoon sugar, and ¼ teaspoon fine sea salt; stir and simmer uncovered for 10 minutes until the sauce thickens and the tomatoes deepen in color.
      9. [ T-46] ( 10m preheat) Cumin-Spiced Roasted Cauliflower
         ⟶ With the Whole Wheat Roti resting, start on the Cumin-Spiced Roasted Cauliflower.
