══════════ COOK MODE (cook-session.tsx: sequenced if multi-dish, flattened if single) ══════════

  ▸ Chipotle-Spiced Pulled Pork Tacos with Pickled Red Onion and Cilantro-Lime Slaw   [Sunday]  SEQUENCED
    footer 132 min | scheduler 132 | card 132 | fresh derive 132 | dishes 3
      1. [T-132] (  4m prep) Chipotle-Spiced Pulled Pork
         Roughly chop the white onion and smash 4 garlic cloves; set aside.
      2. [T-128] (  3m prep) Chipotle-Spiced Pulled Pork
         In a small bowl, combine 1½ teaspoons ground cumin, 1 teaspoon smoked paprika, ½ teaspoon dried oregano, ½ teaspoon garlic powder, 1½ teaspoons kosher salt, and ½ teaspoon black pepper.
      3. [T-125] (  5m prep) Chipotle-Spiced Pulled Pork
         Mince 3 chipotle peppers in adobo and measure 2 tablespoons of the adobo sauce; toss the 2-pound pork shoulder chunks with the spice mixture, minced chipotles, and adobo sauce until evenly coated.
      4. [T-120] ( 16m cook) Chipotle-Spiced Pulled Pork
         Heat 2 tablespoons neutral oil in a large heavy skillet or Dutch oven over medium-high; sear the pork chunks in batches, 3–4 minutes per side, until deeply browned on all sides.
      5. [T-104] ( 90m cook) Chipotle-Spiced Pulled Pork
         Transfer the seared pork to a Dutch oven or heavy pot; add the chopped onion, smashed garlic, ½ cup fresh orange juice, and ½ cup chicken broth, then cover and braise over low heat for 1½ hours until the pork is fall-apart tender.
      6. [T-104] (  8m prep) Cilantro-Lime Slaw
         ⟶ With the Chipotle-Spiced Pulled Pork cooking, start on the Cilantro-Lime Slaw.
         Finely shred 3 cups green cabbage and place in a large bowl.
      7. [ T-96] (  4m prep) Cilantro-Lime Slaw
         Seed and finely mince 1 jalapeño; add it to the bowl with ¼ cup roughly chopped fresh cilantro.
      8. [ T-92] (  3m prep) Cilantro-Lime Slaw
         Whisk together 3 tablespoons fresh lime juice, 2 tablespoons mayonnaise, ¼ teaspoon kosher salt, and ⅛ teaspoon black pepper in a small bowl until smooth.
      9. [ T-89] (  2m assemble) Cilantro-Lime Slaw
         Pour the dressing over the cabbage mixture and toss well to coat; taste and adjust salt if needed, then serve immediately.
     10. [ T-39] (  5m prep) Pickled Red Onion
         ⟶ With the Chipotle-Spiced Pulled Pork cooking, start on the Pickled Red Onion.
         Thinly slice 1 red onion into half-moons and pack them into a heatproof jar or bowl.
     11. [ T-34] (  4m cook) Pickled Red Onion
         Combine ½ cup apple cider vinegar, ¼ cup water, 1 teaspoon granulated sugar, and ½ teaspoon kosher salt in a small saucepan and bring to a boil over medium-high heat, stirring until the sugar and salt dissolve.
     12. [ T-30] ( 30m rest) Pickled Red Onion
         Pour the hot brine over the sliced onions, press them down to submerge, and let them pickle at room temperature for at least 30 minutes before serving.
     13. [ T-14] (  5m cook) Chipotle-Spiced Pulled Pork
         ⟶ With the Pickled Red Onion marinating, start on the Chipotle-Spiced Pulled Pork.
         Transfer the pork to a cutting board and use two forks to shred it; return the shredded pork to the pot and stir it into the braising juices.
     14. [  T-9] (  6m cook) Chipotle-Spiced Pulled Pork
         Warm 12 corn tortillas directly over a gas flame or in a dry skillet, 30 seconds per side, and wrap in a clean towel to keep warm.
     15. [  T-3] (  3m assemble) Chipotle-Spiced Pulled Pork
         Pile the pulled pork into the warm tortillas and garnish with ¼ cup roughly chopped fresh cilantro; serve with lime wedges alongside.

  ▸ Carolina Vinegar Pulled Pork with Tangy Slaw   [Wednesday]  SEQUENCED
    footer 519 min | scheduler 519 | card 519 | fresh derive 519 | dishes 2
      1. [T-519] (  3m prep) Carolina Vinegar Pulled Pork
         Mix 1½ teaspoons kosher salt, 1 teaspoon black pepper, 1 teaspoon smoked paprika, ½ teaspoon garlic powder, ½ teaspoon onion powder, and ¼ teaspoon cayenne together in a small bowl.
      2. [T-516] (  4m prep) Carolina Vinegar Pulled Pork
         Pat the 3-pound bone-in pork shoulder completely dry with paper towels, then coat all sides evenly with the dry rub, pressing it into the meat.
      3. [T-512] (  3m prep) Carolina Vinegar Pulled Pork
         Whisk together ¾ cup apple cider vinegar, ¼ cup water, 1 tablespoon brown sugar, and ½ teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes in a small bowl until the sugar dissolves; set aside.
      4. [T-509] ( 16m cook) Carolina Vinegar Pulled Pork
         Heat 1 tablespoon neutral oil in a large heavy skillet or Dutch oven over medium-high until shimmering, then sear the pork shoulder 3–4 minutes per side on all sides until a deep brown crust forms.
      5. [T-493] (480m cook) Carolina Vinegar Pulled Pork
         Transfer the seared pork shoulder to a slow cooker, pour the vinegar sauce over and around it, cover, and cook on low for 8 hours until the meat is fall-apart tender.
      6. [T-493] ( 10m prep) Creamy Vinegar Cabbage Slaw
         ⟶ With the Carolina Vinegar Pulled Pork cooking, start on the Creamy Vinegar Cabbage Slaw.
         Finely shred ½ head green cabbage and ¼ head red cabbage, then peel and grate 1 large carrot; combine all three in a large bowl.
      7. [T-483] (  3m prep) Creamy Vinegar Cabbage Slaw
         Whisk together 3 tablespoons mayonnaise, 2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar, 1 teaspoon granulated sugar, ¼ teaspoon celery seed, ½ teaspoon kosher salt, and ¼ teaspoon black pepper in a small bowl until smooth.
      8. [T-480] (  2m assemble) Creamy Vinegar Cabbage Slaw
         Pour the dressing over the slaw base and toss thoroughly to coat every strand.
      9. [T-478] ( 20m rest) Creamy Vinegar Cabbage Slaw
         Cover and refrigerate the slaw for at least 20 minutes before serving so the flavors meld and the cabbage softens slightly.
     10. [ T-13] ( 10m rest) Carolina Vinegar Pulled Pork
         Transfer the pork to a cutting board and let it rest for 10 minutes, then use two forks to pull the meat into shreds, discarding the bone and any large fat pockets.
     11. [  T-3] (  3m assemble) Carolina Vinegar Pulled Pork
         Return the pulled pork to the slow cooker and toss it in the cooking juices, then serve piled high alongside the slaw.

         ⟶ With the Vinegar Coleslaw chilling, start on the Alabama White Sauce.
         Squeeze 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice and measure out ½ cup mayonnaise, 2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar, 1 tablespoon prepared horseradish, 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard, ½ teaspoon black pepper, ¼ teaspoon kosher salt, ¼ teaspoon garlic powder, and ½ teaspoon sugar.
     13. [ T-24] (  2m assemble) Alabama White Sauce
         Whisk all measured ingredients together until completely smooth and combined.

         ⟶ With the Vinegar Coleslaw chilling, start on the Slow-Roasted Pulled Pork.

     16. [  T-2] (  2m assemble) Vinegar Coleslaw
         Toss the slaw once more just before serving and adjust seasoning with a pinch of salt if needed.
