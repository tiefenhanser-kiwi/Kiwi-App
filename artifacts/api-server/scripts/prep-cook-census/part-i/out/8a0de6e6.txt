      2. [T-518] (  6m prep) Red Wine and Herb Slow-Cooker Chuck Roast

      3. [T-512] ( 10m cook) Red Wine and Herb Slow-Cooker Chuck Roast
         Heat 2 tablespoons vegetable oil in a large heavy skillet over medium-high until shimmering, then sear the roast 4 minutes per side until a deep brown crust forms.
      4. [T-502] (  4m cook) Red Wine and Herb Slow-Cooker Chuck Roast
         Reduce heat to medium, add the onion and celery to the same skillet, and cook 3 minutes until slightly softened, then stir in 2 tablespoons tomato paste and cook 1 minute more.
      5. [T-498] (  3m cook) Red Wine and Herb Slow-Cooker Chuck Roast
         Pour in ¾ cup dry red wine and scrape up any browned bits from the bottom of the skillet, then cook 2 minutes until slightly reduced.
      6. [T-495] (  2m prep) Red Wine and Herb Slow-Cooker Chuck Roast
         Measure 1 cup beef broth and stir in 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce.
      7. [T-493] (  3m cook) Red Wine and Herb Slow-Cooker Chuck Roast
         Transfer the seared roast to the slow cooker, pour the wine-onion mixture and broth over it, then tuck in the 5 garlic cloves, 4 thyme sprigs, 2 rosemary sprigs, and 2 bay leaves.
      8. [T-490] (480m cook) Red Wine and Herb Slow-Cooker Chuck Roast
         Cover and cook on low for 8 hours (or high for 4½ hours) until the roast is completely fall-apart tender.
      9. [ T-56] ( 15m preheat) Roasted Yukon Gold Potatoes
         ⟶ With the Red Wine and Herb Slow-Cooker Chuck Roast cooking, start on the Roasted Yukon Gold Potatoes.
         Preheat the oven to 425°F and line a large rimmed baking sheet with parchment paper.
     10. [ T-56] (  8m prep) Roasted Yukon Gold Potatoes
         Cut the 1½ pounds Yukon Gold potatoes into 1½-inch chunks and toss on the prepared baking sheet with 2 tablespoons olive oil, ¾ teaspoon kosher salt, ½ teaspoon black pepper, and ½ teaspoon garlic powder until evenly coated.
     11. [ T-42] (  7m prep) Honey-Glazed Roasted Carrots
         ⟶ With the Red Wine and Herb Slow-Cooker Chuck Roast cooking, start on the Honey-Glazed Roasted Carrots.
         Peel the 1 pound carrots and cut them into 2-inch batons of roughly equal thickness.
     12. [ T-35] ( 33m cook) Roasted Yukon Gold Potatoes
         ⟶ With the Red Wine and Herb Slow-Cooker Chuck Roast cooking, start on the Roasted Yukon Gold Potatoes.
         Spread the potatoes in a single layer and roast at 425°F for 30–35 minutes, flipping once halfway through, until golden and crisp on the edges.
     13. [ T-35] (  4m prep) Honey-Glazed Roasted Carrots
         ⟶ With the Red Wine and Herb Slow-Cooker Chuck Roast cooking, start on the Honey-Glazed Roasted Carrots.
         In a large bowl, whisk together 1½ tablespoons olive oil, 1 tablespoon honey, ½ teaspoon kosher salt, ¼ teaspoon black pepper, and 1 teaspoon fresh thyme leaves, then toss the carrots in the glaze until fully coated.
     14. [ T-31] ( 24m cook) Honey-Glazed Roasted Carrots

     15. [ T-10] (  4m assemble) Red Wine and Herb Slow-Cooker Chuck Roast

  ▸ Biscuits and Sausage Gravy with Scrambled Eggs   [Tuesday]  SEQUENCED
    footer 31 min | scheduler 31 | card 31 | fresh derive 31 | dishes 3
      1. [ T-31] ( 10m preheat) Biscuits
         Preheat the oven to 375°F (or the temperature on the Pillsbury Grands can).
      2. [ T-26] (  7m cook) Sausage Gravy
         ⟶ With the Biscuits heating up, start on the Sausage Gravy.
         Cook 1 pound of bulk breakfast sausage in a large skillet over medium-high heat, breaking it up with a wooden spoon, for 6–7 minutes until browned and cooked through with no pink remaining.
      3. [ T-19] ( 14m cook) Biscuits
         Pop open the 16.3 oz can, separate the 8 biscuits, and arrange them 2 inches apart on an ungreased baking sheet; bake 13–15 minutes until deep golden brown.
      4. [ T-19] (  2m cook) Sausage Gravy
         ⟶ With the Biscuits cooking, start on the Sausage Gravy.
         Reduce the heat to medium, sprinkle ¼ cup all-purpose flour evenly over the sausage, and stir constantly for 2 minutes to cook out the raw flour taste.
      5. [ T-17] (  6m cook) Sausage Gravy
         Pour in 2½ cups whole milk in a steady stream, stirring constantly; cook over medium heat for 5–6 minutes, stirring frequently, until the gravy is thick and bubbling.
      6. [ T-11] (  3m prep) Scrambled Eggs
         ⟶ With the Biscuits cooking, start on the Scrambled Eggs.
         Crack 8 large eggs into a bowl, add 3 tablespoons whole milk, ¼ teaspoon kosher salt, and ⅛ teaspoon black pepper, and whisk until the yolks and whites are fully combined and slightly frothy.
      7. [  T-8] (  5m hold) Sausage Gravy
         ⟶ With the Biscuits cooking, start on the Sausage Gravy.
         Season with ¾ teaspoon black pepper, ¼ teaspoon kosher salt, and ¼ teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes (optional); taste and adjust seasoning, then reduce heat to the lowest setting to keep warm.
      8. [  T-8] (  4m cook) Scrambled Eggs
         ⟶ With the Biscuits cooking, start on the Scrambled Eggs.
         Melt 1 tablespoon unsalted butter in a nonstick skillet over medium-low heat, pour in the egg mixture, and cook for 3–4 minutes, folding gently with a silicone spatula every 30 seconds, pulling the curds from the edges until just set and still glossy.
      9. [  T-4] (  2m assemble) Biscuits
         ⟶ With the Sausage Gravy staying warm, start on the Biscuits.
         Transfer the biscuits to a wire rack and split each one in half before serving.
     10. [  T-2] (  1m assemble) Sausage Gravy
         Ladle the gravy generously over the split biscuits and serve immediately.
     11. [  T-1] (  1m assemble) Scrambled Eggs
         Slide the eggs onto a plate and scatter 2 tablespoons thinly sliced fresh chives over the top (optional).

      8. [ T-28] (  2m cook) Ground Turkey and Tomato Meat Sauce

     10. [ T-26] ( 10m preheat) Spaghetti
