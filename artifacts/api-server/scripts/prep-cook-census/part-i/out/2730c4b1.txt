      1. [ T-57] (  4m prep) Spiced Beef Kofta
         Grate half a yellow onion on the large holes of a box grater, then squeeze out as much liquid as possible with your hands and discard the liquid.
      2. [ T-53] (  4m prep) Spiced Beef Kofta
         Finely chop ¼ cup flat-leaf parsley and mince 3 garlic cloves.
      3. [ T-49] (  4m prep) Pickled Onions and Charred Flatbread
         Thinly slice 1 red onion into half-moons.
      4. [ T-45] (  2m prep) Spiced Beef Kofta
         In a large bowl, combine 1½ teaspoons cumin, 1 teaspoon coriander, 1 teaspoon smoked paprika, ¼ teaspoon cinnamon, and ¼ teaspoon cayenne.
      5. [ T-43] (  4m prep) Spiced Beef Kofta
         Add the grated onion, parsley, garlic, spices, 1 teaspoon kosher salt, and ½ teaspoon black pepper to 1½ pounds ground beef and mix firmly with your hands until fully combined — do not overwork.
      6. [ T-43] (  3m cook) Pickled Onions and Charred Flatbread
         In a small saucepan, combine ½ cup red wine vinegar, ¼ cup water, 1 teaspoon sugar, and ½ teaspoon kosher salt; bring to a simmer over medium heat, stirring until the sugar and salt dissolve, about 2 minutes.
      7. [ T-39] ( 20m rest) Pickled Onions and Charred Flatbread
         Pour the hot brine over the sliced onions in a heatproof bowl or jar, press them down to submerge, and let sit at room temperature for at least 20 minutes.
      8. [ T-39] (  2m prep) Pickled Onions and Charred Flatbread
         Brush both sides of 4 flatbreads with 2 tablespoons olive oil.
      9. [ T-37] (  8m cook) Pickled Onions and Charred Flatbread
         Heat a dry cast-iron skillet or grill pan over high heat and char the flatbreads one at a time, 1–2 minutes per side, until dark grill marks appear and the edges blister.
     10. [ T-29] (  6m prep) Spiced Beef Kofta
         ⟶ With the Pickled Onions and Charred Flatbread marinating, start on the Spiced Beef Kofta.
         Divide the mixture into 12 equal portions and shape each into a 4-inch oval patty about ¾ inch thick; brush both sides lightly with 1 tablespoon olive oil.
     11. [ T-23] (  2m preheat) Spiced Beef Kofta
         Heat a large cast-iron skillet or grill pan over medium-high heat for 2 minutes until very hot.
     12. [ T-23] (  5m hold) Pickled Onions and Charred Flatbread
         ⟶ With the Spiced Beef Kofta cooking, start on the Pickled Onions and Charred Flatbread.
         Stack the charred flatbreads and wrap loosely in foil to keep warm while finishing the kofta.
     13. [ T-21] (  8m cook) Spiced Beef Kofta
         ⟶ With the Pickled Onions and Charred Flatbread marinating, start on the Spiced Beef Kofta.
         Cook the kofta in batches without crowding, 3–4 minutes per side, until deeply browned and cooked through to 160 °F — do not press or move them until ready to flip.
     14. [ T-13] (  3m prep) Creamy Hummus
         Drain two 15-oz cans of chickpeas, reserving the liquid, and add the chickpeas to a food processor with 2 garlic cloves; process for 1 minute until broken down.
     15. [ T-10] (  3m rest) Spiced Beef Kofta
         Transfer the cooked kofta to a plate and rest for 3 minutes before serving.
     16. [ T-10] (  2m prep) Creamy Hummus
         ⟶ With the Spiced Beef Kofta resting, start on the Creamy Hummus.
         Add ⅓ cup tahini, 3 tablespoons fresh lemon juice, ½ teaspoon cumin, and ¾ teaspoon kosher salt; process for 1 minute.
     17. [  T-8] (  4m prep) Creamy Hummus
         With the processor running, drizzle in 3 tablespoons olive oil, then add 3 tablespoons of the reserved chickpea liquid (or ice water) one tablespoon at a time; process for 2–3 minutes until very smooth and creamy.
     18. [  T-4] (  2m assemble) Creamy Hummus
         Spread the hummus into a wide, shallow bowl using the back of a spoon to create a deep well in the center, then drizzle with olive oil.
     19. [  T-2] (  2m assemble) Pickled Onions and Charred Flatbread
         Drain the pickled onions and pile them alongside the kofta and hummus; garnish with 2 tablespoons roughly chopped parsley and ½ teaspoon sumac (both optional) and serve immediately with the charred flatbread.

  ▸ Stuffed Grape Leaves with Tzatziki and Warm Pita   [Tuesday]  SEQUENCED
    footer 102 min | scheduler 102 | card 102 | fresh derive 102 | dishes 3
      1. [T-102] (  8m prep) Herb and Rice Stuffed Grape Leaves (Dolmades)
         Rinse 40 grape leaves under cold water, pat dry, and lay flat on a clean work surface; trim any tough stems flush with the leaf.
      2. [ T-94] ( 10m prep) Herb and Rice Stuffed Grape Leaves (Dolmades)
         Finely dice the yellow onion, mince 3 cloves of garlic, finely chop ½ cup flat-leaf parsley, ¼ cup mint leaves, and 2 tablespoons fresh dill; juice 1 lemon and slice the second lemon into thin rounds.
      3. [ T-84] (  5m prep) Herb and Rice Stuffed Grape Leaves (Dolmades)
         In a large bowl, combine 1 pound ground lamb, 1 cup uncooked long-grain white rice, the diced onion, minced garlic, parsley, mint, dill, the juice of 1 lemon, 3 tablespoons olive oil, ½ teaspoon allspice, ¼ teaspoon cinnamon, 1 teaspoon kosher salt, and ½ teaspoon black pepper; mix until just combined.
      4. [ T-79] ( 18m assemble) Herb and Rice Stuffed Grape Leaves (Dolmades)
         Place a grape leaf vein-side up, add 1 heaping tablespoon of filling near the stem end, fold the sides in, and roll tightly into a small cylinder; repeat with remaining leaves and filling.
      5. [ T-61] (  3m assemble) Herb and Rice Stuffed Grape Leaves (Dolmades)
         Line the bottom of a large, heavy pot with 4–5 flat grape leaves to prevent sticking, then arrange the dolmades seam-side down in tight snug layers.
      6. [ T-58] (  3m cook) Herb and Rice Stuffed Grape Leaves (Dolmades)
         Lay the lemon slices over the top layer of dolmades, pour in 1½ cups low-sodium chicken broth, and drizzle with any remaining olive oil; place a heavy plate on top to keep the rolls from unraveling.
