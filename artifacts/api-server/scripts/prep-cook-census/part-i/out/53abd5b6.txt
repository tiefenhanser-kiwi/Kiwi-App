  ▸ Classic BBQ Chicken Pizza with Red Onion and Cilantro   [Sunday]  FLATTENED (single-dish)
    footer 70 min | scheduler 60 | card 60 | fresh derive 60 | dishes 1
      1. [ T-60] (  2m prep) 
         Pull the store-bought pizza dough from the fridge and let it sit at room temperature for at least 30 minutes so it stretches easily.
      2. [ T-58] (  3m prep) 
         Season the ¾ pound boneless skinless chicken breasts on both sides with ½ teaspoon kosher salt, ¼ teaspoon black pepper, and ½ teaspoon garlic powder.
      3. [    —] ( 17m cook) 
         Heat a lightly oiled skillet over medium-high and cook the seasoned chicken breasts 6 minutes per side until cooked through, then transfer to a cutting board and let rest 5 minutes before shredding with two forks.
      4. [ T-55] (  2m prep) 
         Toss the shredded chicken with ⅓ cup of the BBQ sauce in a bowl until evenly coated and set aside.
      5. [ T-38] (  5m prep) 
         Thinly slice the ½ red onion into half-moons and shred the 8 oz mozzarella and 2 oz smoked gouda if not pre-shredded.
      6. [ T-36] ( 15m preheat) 
         Place a rack in the lowest position and preheat the oven to 475°F (or as high as your oven goes), setting a baking sheet or pizza stone inside to heat.
      7. [ T-31] (  5m prep) 
         On a lightly floured surface, stretch and press the 16 oz pizza dough into a roughly 12-inch round, then transfer it to a piece of parchment paper.
      8. [ T-31] (  2m assemble) 
         Spread the remaining ⅓ cup BBQ sauce evenly over the dough, leaving a ¾-inch border, then brush the border with 1 tablespoon olive oil.
      9. [ T-26] (  3m assemble) 
         Scatter the shredded mozzarella and smoked gouda evenly over the sauce, then distribute the BBQ chicken and red onion slices across the top; sprinkle with crushed red pepper flakes if using.
     10. [ T-24] ( 14m cook) 
         Slide the pizza (on its parchment) onto the preheated baking sheet or stone and bake at 475°F for 12–15 minutes until the crust is deep golden and the cheese is bubbling and lightly browned in spots.
     11. [ T-16] (  2m assemble) 
         Remove the pizza from the oven, scatter ¼ cup fresh cilantro leaves over the top, slice into 8 pieces, and serve immediately.

  ▸ Buffalo Chicken Salad with Crispy Chicken, Romaine, Celery, Blue Cheese, and Ranch   [Thursday]  FLATTENED (single-dish)
    footer 40 min | scheduler 40 | card 40 | fresh derive 40 | dishes 1
      1. [ T-40] (  5m prep) 
         Slice 1¾ pounds boneless skinless chicken breasts into 1-inch strips and pat dry with paper towels.
      2. [ T-35] (  3m prep) 
         In a shallow bowl, whisk together ½ cup all-purpose flour, ½ teaspoon garlic powder, ½ teaspoon onion powder, ½ teaspoon smoked paprika, ¾ teaspoon kosher salt, and ¼ teaspoon black pepper.
      3. [ T-32] (  4m prep) 
         Dredge each chicken strip in the seasoned flour, pressing to coat evenly, and shake off any excess.
      4. [ T-28] ( 10m cook) 
         Heat 3 tablespoons vegetable oil in a large skillet over medium-high until shimmering, then sear the chicken strips in a single layer for 3–4 minutes per side until golden and cooked through — work in batches to avoid crowding.
      5. [    —] (  3m cook) 
         In a small saucepan over low heat, melt 2 tablespoons unsalted butter with ½ cup Frank's RedHot sauce, stirring until smooth and combined, about 2 minutes.
      6. [    —] (  8m prep) 
         Chop 3 romaine lettuce hearts into bite-sized pieces, thinly slice 4 celery stalks on the bias, halve 1 cup cherry tomatoes, and thinly slice ½ red onion.
      7. [ T-18] (  2m assemble) 
         Toss the cooked chicken strips in the buffalo sauce until evenly coated.
      8. [    —] (  3m assemble) 
         Divide the romaine, celery, cherry tomatoes, and red onion among 4 serving bowls.
      9. [ T-15] (  2m assemble) 
         Top each bowl with the buffalo chicken strips, 1 oz blue cheese crumbles, and 2 tablespoons ranch dressing, then garnish with fresh chives (optional) and serve immediately.
