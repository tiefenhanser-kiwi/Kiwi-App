  ▸ Smoky Chipotle Carne Asada Tacos with Pico de Gallo and Cilantro-Lime Slaw   [Monday]  SEQUENCED
    footer 50 min | scheduler 50 | card 50 | fresh derive 50 | dishes 3
      1. [ T-50] (  8m prep) Smoky Chipotle Carne Asada
         Mince 2 chipotle chiles in adobo plus 4 garlic cloves, then whisk together with 1 tablespoon adobo sauce, ¼ cup fresh orange juice, 3 tablespoons fresh lime juice, 2 tablespoons olive oil, 1 teaspoon ground cumin, 1 teaspoon smoked paprika, ½ teaspoon dried oregano, 1 teaspoon kosher salt, and ½ teaspoon black pepper to form the marinade.
      2. [ T-42] ( 20m rest) Smoky Chipotle Carne Asada
         Add the 1½ pounds of trimmed skirt steak to the marinade, turn to coat thoroughly, and let it marinate for at least 20 minutes at room temperature (or up to 4 hours refrigerated).
      3. [ T-42] (  3m preheat) Smoky Chipotle Carne Asada
         Heat a cast-iron skillet or grill pan over high heat until smoking hot, about 3 minutes.
      4. [ T-42] ( 10m prep) Fresh Pico de Gallo
         ⟶ With the Smoky Chipotle Carne Asada chilling, start on the Fresh Pico de Gallo.
         Core and finely dice 3 roma tomatoes, finely dice ½ white onion, seed and mince 1 jalapeño, and finely chop ¼ cup fresh cilantro.
      5. [ T-32] (  8m prep) Cilantro-Lime Slaw
         ⟶ With the Smoky Chipotle Carne Asada chilling, start on the Cilantro-Lime Slaw.
         Thinly shred 3 cups green cabbage and 1 cup red cabbage and place in a large bowl.
      6. [ T-24] (  3m prep) Cilantro-Lime Slaw
         Whisk together 2 tablespoons fresh lime juice, 2 tablespoons mayonnaise, 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar, 1 teaspoon honey, and ¼ teaspoon kosher salt in a small bowl until smooth.
      7. [ T-21] (  2m assemble) Fresh Pico de Gallo
         Combine the tomatoes, onion, jalapeño, and cilantro in a bowl, add 2 tablespoons fresh lime juice and ½ teaspoon kosher salt, and stir to combine.
      8. [ T-19] (  2m assemble) Cilantro-Lime Slaw
         Add 3 tablespoons chopped fresh cilantro to the cabbage, pour the dressing over the top, and toss until everything is evenly coated.
      9. [ T-17] (  8m cook) Smoky Chipotle Carne Asada
         Remove the steak from the marinade, shaking off excess, and sear for 3–4 minutes per side for medium-rare, pressing lightly to ensure full contact — do not move the steak between flips.
     10. [ T-17] ( 10m rest) Fresh Pico de Gallo
         Let the pico sit for at least 10 minutes before serving so the flavors meld.
     11. [ T-17] ( 10m rest) Cilantro-Lime Slaw
         ⟶ With the Fresh Pico de Gallo resting, start on the Cilantro-Lime Slaw.
         Refrigerate the slaw for at least 10 minutes before serving to allow the cabbage to soften slightly and the flavors to come together.
     12. [  T-9] (  5m rest) Smoky Chipotle Carne Asada
         ⟶ With the Fresh Pico de Gallo resting, start on the Smoky Chipotle Carne Asada.
         Transfer the steak to a cutting board and rest for 5 minutes.
     13. [  T-9] (  5m cook) Smoky Chipotle Carne Asada
         While the steak rests, warm the 12 corn tortillas in a dry skillet over medium heat or directly over a gas flame, about 30 seconds per side, then stack and wrap in a clean towel to keep warm.
     14. [  T-4] (  4m assemble) Smoky Chipotle Carne Asada
         Slice the rested steak against the grain into thin strips, then pile into the warm tortillas and top with the finely diced ¼ white onion, ¼ cup roughly chopped fresh cilantro, pico de gallo, slaw, and a squeeze of lime wedge.

  ▸ Carnitas Tacos with Charred Corn Salsa and Jalapeño Slaw   [Tuesday]  SEQUENCED
    footer 94 min | scheduler 94 | card 94 | fresh derive 94 | dishes 3
      1. [ T-94] (  3m prep) Citrus-Braised Pork Carnitas
         In a small bowl, combine 1½ teaspoons ground cumin, 1 teaspoon dried oregano, 1 teaspoon chili powder, 1½ teaspoons kosher salt, and ½ teaspoon black pepper.
      2. [ T-91] (  3m prep) Citrus-Braised Pork Carnitas
         Toss the 2 pounds of pork shoulder chunks with the spice blend until evenly coated.
      3. [ T-88] (  2m preheat) Citrus-Braised Pork Carnitas
         Heat 1 tablespoon vegetable oil in a large, heavy-bottomed pot or Dutch oven over medium-high heat until shimmering.
      4. [ T-86] ( 12m cook) Citrus-Braised Pork Carnitas
         Sear the pork in a single layer, undisturbed, for 3–4 minutes per side until deeply browned on at least two sides — work in batches to avoid crowding.
      5. [ T-74] ( 32m cook) Citrus-Braised Pork Carnitas
         Add the juice of 1 orange, the juice of 1 lime, 4 smashed garlic cloves, and ½ cup water to the pot; bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and braise for 30 minutes until the pork is tender and pulls apart easily.
      6. [ T-74] (  7m prep) Jalapeño Slaw
         ⟶ With the Citrus-Braised Pork Carnitas marinating, start on the Jalapeño Slaw.
         Thinly shred 2 cups green cabbage and peel and julienne or coarsely grate 1 carrot.
      7. [ T-67] (  2m prep) Jalapeño Slaw
         Seed and thinly slice 1 jalapeño and add it to the bowl with the slaw base.
      8. [ T-65] (  3m prep) Jalapeño Slaw
         In a small bowl, whisk together 2 tablespoons mayonnaise, 2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar, 1 teaspoon honey, ¼ teaspoon kosher salt, and ⅛ teaspoon black pepper until smooth.
      9. [ T-62] ( 10m rest) Jalapeño Slaw
         Pour the dressing over the slaw, toss well to coat, and refrigerate for at least 10 minutes before serving to let the flavors meld.
     10. [ T-42] ( 10m cook) Citrus-Braised Pork Carnitas
         Uncover, increase heat to medium-high, and cook for 8–10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the braising liquid has nearly evaporated and the pork is crisping in its own fat.
     11. [ T-32] (  5m prep) Charred Corn Salsa
         Finely dice 1 roma tomato and ¼ red onion, and chop 2 tablespoons fresh cilantro; set aside.
     12. [ T-27] (  3m assemble) Citrus-Braised Pork Carnitas
         Use two forks to roughly shred the carnitas directly in the pot, tossing to coat all pieces in the caramelized drippings.
     13. [ T-24] (  2m preheat) Charred Corn Salsa
         Heat 1 teaspoon vegetable oil in a cast-iron skillet or grill pan over high heat until smoking.
     14. [ T-22] (  8m cook) Charred Corn Salsa
         Stand each husked ear of corn upright and char it in the dry hot skillet, turning every 2 minutes, until kernels are blistered and golden in spots, about 8 minutes total; alternatively, spread 1½ cups thawed frozen corn in the skillet and cook undisturbed for 3–4 minutes until charred.
     15. [ T-14] (  3m prep) Charred Corn Salsa
         Once cool enough to handle, cut the kernels from the cobs with a sharp knife.
     16. [ T-11] (  5m cook) Citrus-Braised Pork Carnitas
         Warm the 12 corn tortillas directly over a gas flame or in a dry skillet for 30 seconds per side, then stack and wrap in a clean kitchen towel to keep warm.
     17. [  T-6] (  3m assemble) Citrus-Braised Pork Carnitas
         Pile the carnitas into the warm tortillas and finish with roughly chopped fresh cilantro (optional) and a squeeze from the lime wedges.
     18. [  T-3] (  3m assemble) Charred Corn Salsa
         Combine the charred corn, diced tomato, red onion, and cilantro in a bowl; squeeze over the juice of 1 lime, add ¼ teaspoon kosher salt, and toss to combine.
