  ▸ Blackened Shrimp Tacos with Mango Salsa and Lime Crema   [Monday]  SEQUENCED
    footer 39 min | scheduler 39 | card 39 | fresh derive 39 | dishes 3
      1. [ T-39] (  3m prep) Blackened Shrimp
         In a small bowl, combine 1½ teaspoons smoked paprika, 1 teaspoon garlic powder, ½ teaspoon onion powder, ½ teaspoon dried oregano, ½ teaspoon cayenne pepper, ½ teaspoon ground cumin, ¾ teaspoon kosher salt, and ¼ teaspoon black pepper; stir to combine.
      2. [ T-36] (  4m prep) Blackened Shrimp
         Pat 1½ pounds of peeled, deveined shrimp thoroughly dry with paper towels, then toss them in the blackening spice blend until evenly coated.
      3. [ T-32] (  8m prep) Fresh Mango Salsa
         Peel and finely dice 2 ripe mangoes, finely dice ¼ of a red onion, and seed and mince 1 jalapeño.
      4. [ T-24] (  3m prep) Lime Crema
         Zest 1 teaspoon of lime zest and squeeze 1½ tablespoons of fresh lime juice.
      5. [ T-21] (  2m preheat) Blackened Shrimp
         Heat 2 tablespoons neutral oil in a large cast-iron or heavy skillet over high heat until the oil shimmers and just begins to smoke.
      6. [ T-21] (  2m assemble) Lime Crema
         ⟶ With the Blackened Shrimp cooking, start on the Lime Crema.
         Whisk together ½ cup sour cream, 2 tablespoons mayonnaise, the lime zest, 1½ tablespoons lime juice, ¼ teaspoon garlic powder, and ⅛ teaspoon kosher salt until smooth.
      7. [ T-19] (  4m cook) Blackened Shrimp
         Add the shrimp in a single layer and cook undisturbed for 2 minutes until a dark crust forms on the bottom, then flip each shrimp and cook 1–2 minutes more until just opaque and cooked through.
      8. [ T-15] (  3m prep) Fresh Mango Salsa
         Finely chop 3 tablespoons of fresh cilantro and squeeze 2 tablespoons of fresh lime juice.
      9. [ T-12] (  5m cook) Blackened Shrimp
         Warm 12 corn tortillas directly over a gas flame or in a dry skillet for about 30 seconds per side until pliable and lightly charred.
     10. [ T-12] ( 10m rest) Lime Crema
         Refrigerate the crema until ready to serve, or leave at room temperature if serving within 20 minutes.
     11. [  T-7] (  2m assemble) Fresh Mango Salsa
         ⟶ With the Lime Crema chilling, start on the Fresh Mango Salsa.
         Combine the mango, red onion, jalapeño, cilantro, lime juice, and ¼ teaspoon kosher salt in a bowl and toss gently to combine.
     12. [  T-5] (  3m assemble) Blackened Shrimp
         ⟶ With the Lime Crema chilling, start on the Blackened Shrimp.
         Divide the blackened shrimp among the warm tortillas, top with mango salsa and lime crema, scatter ¼ cup cilantro leaves over the top, and serve with lime wedges alongside.
     13. [  T-5] (  5m rest) Fresh Mango Salsa
         ⟶ With the Lime Crema chilling, start on the Fresh Mango Salsa.
         Taste and adjust salt or lime juice as needed, then set aside at room temperature until ready to serve (up to 30 minutes).

      1. [ T-52] (  5m prep) Chipotle-Roasted Chicken Thighs
         In a large bowl, whisk together 2 minced chipotle peppers, 1 tablespoon adobo sauce, 2 tablespoons olive oil, 3 minced garlic cloves, 1 teaspoon ground cumin, 1 teaspoon smoked paprika, ½ teaspoon dried oregano, 1 teaspoon kosher salt, ½ teaspoon black pepper, and 2 tablespoons lime juice into a marinade.
      2. [ T-47] ( 15m rest) Chipotle-Roasted Chicken Thighs
         Add 2 pounds boneless skinless chicken thighs to the marinade, toss to coat thoroughly, and let sit for at least 15 minutes at room temperature (or up to 4 hours refrigerated).
      3. [ T-47] ( 10m preheat) Chipotle-Roasted Chicken Thighs
         Preheat the oven to 425°F and line a rimmed baking sheet with foil.
      4. [ T-34] (  4m prep) Cilantro-Lime Rice
         ⟶ With the Chipotle-Roasted Chicken Thighs chilling, start on the Cilantro-Lime Rice.
         Zest and juice 1 lime, measure out 1 teaspoon zest and 2 tablespoons juice, and roughly chop ⅓ cup fresh cilantro; set aside.
      5. [ T-30] ( 24m cook) Chipotle-Roasted Chicken Thighs
         Arrange the marinated chicken thighs in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet and roast at 425°F for 22–25 minutes, until an instant-read thermometer reads 165°F and the edges are slightly caramelized.
      6. [ T-30] (  4m cook) Cilantro-Lime Rice
         ⟶ With the Chipotle-Roasted Chicken Thighs marinating, start on the Cilantro-Lime Rice.
         Combine 1½ cups long-grain white rice, 2¼ cups water, and ¾ teaspoon kosher salt in a medium saucepan and bring to a boil over high heat.
      7. [ T-26] ( 15m cook) Cilantro-Lime Rice
         Reduce heat to low, cover tightly, and cook for 15 minutes until all the water is absorbed.
      8. [ T-26] (  6m prep) Street Corn Salsa and Black Beans
         ⟶ With the Chipotle-Roasted Chicken Thighs marinating, start on the Street Corn Salsa and Black Beans.
         Thaw and pat 2 cups frozen corn kernels thoroughly dry with paper towels; drain and rinse one 15-ounce can of black beans, then finely dice ¼ cup red onion and, if using, seed and finely dice 1 jalapeño.
      9. [ T-20] (  5m cook) Street Corn Salsa and Black Beans
         Heat 1 tablespoon neutral oil in a large skillet over high heat until shimmering, add the corn in a single layer, and cook undisturbed for 3 minutes, then stir and cook 2 more minutes until deeply charred in spots.
     10. [ T-15] (  2m cook) Street Corn Salsa and Black Beans
         Push the corn to one side of the skillet, add the drained black beans to the empty side with ½ teaspoon ground cumin, ¼ teaspoon garlic powder, and ½ teaspoon kosher salt, and stir the beans over the heat for 2 minutes until warmed through and fragrant.
     11. [ T-13] (  3m rest) Street Corn Salsa and Black Beans
         Transfer the charred corn to a bowl and let it cool for 3 minutes, keeping the seasoned beans warm in the skillet over the lowest heat setting.
     12. [ T-11] (  5m rest) Cilantro-Lime Rice
         ⟶ With the Chipotle-Roasted Chicken Thighs marinating, start on the Cilantro-Lime Rice.
         Remove from heat and let the rice steam, covered, for 5 minutes.
     13. [ T-10] (  4m assemble) Street Corn Salsa and Black Beans
         ⟶ With the Chipotle-Roasted Chicken Thighs marinating, start on the Street Corn Salsa and Black Beans.
         Stir 2 tablespoons mayonnaise, 1 tablespoon lime juice, and ½ teaspoon chili powder into the cooled corn, then fold in 3 ounces crumbled cotija, ¼ cup chopped cilantro, the diced red onion, and the jalapeño (if using) to finish the street corn salsa.
     14. [  T-6] (  5m rest) Chipotle-Roasted Chicken Thighs
         Rest the chicken on the baking sheet for 5 minutes, then slice each thigh against the grain into thick strips for serving.
     15. [  T-6] (  3m assemble) Cilantro-Lime Rice
         ⟶ With the Chipotle-Roasted Chicken Thighs resting, start on the Cilantro-Lime Rice.
         Fluff the rice with a fork, then fold in 1 tablespoon unsalted butter, 1 teaspoon lime zest, 2 tablespoons lime juice, and ⅓ cup chopped cilantro until evenly combined.
     16. [  T-3] (  3m assemble) Street Corn Salsa and Black Beans
         ⟶ With the Chipotle-Roasted Chicken Thighs resting, start on the Street Corn Salsa and Black Beans.
         Spoon cilantro-lime rice into each bowl, top with sliced chipotle chicken, a generous scoop of street corn salsa, and the seasoned black beans alongside.
