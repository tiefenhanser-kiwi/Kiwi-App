      5. [ T-60] (  4m cook) Chipotle-Braised Beef
         Remove the seared beef and reduce heat to medium; add the diced onion to the pot and cook, stirring occasionally, for 4 minutes until softened.
      6. [ T-56] (  1m cook) Chipotle-Braised Beef
         Add the minced garlic, minced chipotles, adobo sauce, and the spice blend to the pot and stir for 1 minute until fragrant.
      7. [ T-55] ( 48m cook) Chipotle-Braised Beef
         Return the beef to the pot and pour in 1 cup beef broth and ½ cup crushed tomatoes; stir to combine, bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and braise for 45 minutes until the beef is fork-tender.
      8. [ T-35] (  4m prep) Cilantro-Lime Rice
