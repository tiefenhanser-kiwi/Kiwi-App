  ▸ Cajun Shrimp Pasta in Spicy Cream Sauce   [Sunday]  FLATTENED (single-dish)
    footer 53 min | scheduler 33 | card 33 | fresh derive 33 | dishes 1
      1. [ T-33] (  8m preheat) 
         Bring a large pot of heavily salted water to a boil.
      2. [ T-33] (  7m prep) 
         Finely dice the yellow onion, thinly slice the red bell pepper, and mince the 5 garlic cloves.
      3. [ T-26] (  3m prep) 
         In a small bowl, combine 1½ teaspoons smoked paprika, ½ teaspoon cayenne pepper, ¾ teaspoon dried oregano, ½ teaspoon dried thyme, ½ teaspoon onion powder, ½ teaspoon garlic powder, 1¼ teaspoons kosher salt, and ½ teaspoon black pepper to make the Cajun spice blend.
      4. [    —] (  3m prep) 
         Pat the 1½ pounds shrimp dry and toss with half the Cajun spice blend until evenly coated; set aside.
      5. [ T-23] ( 10m cook) 
         Cook the 12 ounces fettuccine in the boiling salted water according to package directions until al dente, then scoop out ½ cup pasta cooking water before draining; set both aside.
      6. [ T-20] (  4m cook) 
         While the pasta cooks, melt 1 tablespoon butter with 1 tablespoon olive oil in a large deep skillet over medium-high heat; add the seasoned shrimp in a single layer and sear 1½ minutes per side until just pink and lightly charred, then transfer to a plate.
      7. [ T-20] (  4m cook) 
         Reduce heat to medium, add the remaining 1 tablespoon butter to the same skillet, then sauté the diced onion and sliced red bell pepper for 4 minutes until softened.
      8. [ T-16] (  1m cook) 
         Add the minced garlic and the remaining Cajun spice blend and cook, stirring constantly, for 1 minute until fragrant.
      9. [ T-12] (  2m cook) 
         Pour in ½ cup dry white wine and scrape up any browned bits from the pan; simmer for 2 minutes until reduced by half.
     10. [ T-11] (  5m cook) 
         Stir in 1½ cups heavy cream, ½ cup chicken broth, and ½ cup crushed tomatoes; bring to a gentle simmer and cook for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sauce thickens slightly.
     11. [ T-11] (  2m cook) 
         Add the drained fettuccine to the skillet and toss to coat, splashing in the reserved pasta cooking water a little at a time until the sauce clings loosely to the noodles.
     12. [  T-6] (  2m assemble) 
         Remove from heat, stir in the 2 ounces finely grated Parmesan until melted, then nestle the seared shrimp back into the pasta and toss gently.
     13. [  T-4] (  2m assemble) 
         Divide among four bowls, scatter ¼ cup roughly chopped flat-leaf parsley over the top, add extra Parmesan, and serve with lemon wedges alongside.

  ▸ Smoky Chipotle Flank Steak Fajitas with Peppers and Onions   [Tuesday]  FLATTENED (single-dish)
    footer 66 min | scheduler 49 | card 49 | fresh derive 49 | dishes 1
      1. [ T-49] (  6m prep) 
         Mince 2 chipotle peppers in adobo and 3 garlic cloves, then whisk together with 1 tablespoon adobo sauce, 3 tablespoons lime juice, 1 teaspoon cumin, 1 teaspoon smoked paprika, ½ teaspoon dried oregano, 1 teaspoon kosher salt, and ½ teaspoon black pepper to form the marinade.
      2. [    —] ( 25m rest) 
         Add the 1½ lb flank steak to the marinade, turn to coat, cover, and let it marinate at room temperature for 20–30 minutes (or refrigerate up to 8 hours).
      3. [ T-43] (  7m prep) 
         Seed and slice 2 red bell peppers and 1 yellow bell pepper into strips, and halve and slice 1 yellow onion into half-moons.
      4. [ T-43] (  8m cook) 
         Heat 1 tablespoon olive oil in a large cast-iron skillet over high heat until just smoking, then add the peppers and onions and cook, stirring occasionally, for 6–8 minutes until charred at the edges and tender; transfer to a plate.
      5. [    —] (  9m cook) 
         Remove the steak from the marinade, pat lightly dry, and add the remaining 1 tablespoon olive oil to the same skillet over high heat; sear the steak 4 minutes per side for medium-rare, or until it reaches your desired doneness.
      6. [ T-36] (  5m rest) 
         Transfer the steak to a cutting board and rest for 5 minutes.
      7. [ T-18] (  2m hold) 
         Wrap the 8 flour tortillas in a damp paper towel and microwave for 45–60 seconds until warm and pliable, then keep wrapped until serving.
      8. [  T-9] (  4m assemble) 
         Slice the rested steak thinly against the grain, then pile it onto the warm tortillas with the charred peppers and onions, top with ½ cup fresh salsa, ½ cup sour cream, and ¼ cup roughly chopped cilantro, and serve with lime wedges alongside.

  ▸ Chicken Fajitas with Roasted Poblano and Corn   [Thursday]  FLATTENED (single-dish)
    footer 38 min | scheduler 38 | card 38 | fresh derive 38 | dishes 1
      1. [ T-38] (  3m prep) 
         In a large bowl, combine 1½ teaspoons ground cumin, 1½ teaspoons chili powder, 1 teaspoon smoked paprika, ½ teaspoon dried oregano, 1 teaspoon kosher salt, and ½ teaspoon black pepper.
      2. [    —] (  8m prep) 
         Halve and thinly slice the white onion, seed and slice the red bell pepper into strips, and seed and slice the 3 poblano peppers into strips.
      3. [ T-35] (  5m prep) 
         Add 1¾ pounds chicken breast strips, 3 minced garlic cloves, 2 tablespoons olive oil, and 2 tablespoons lime juice to the seasoning bowl and toss well to coat; set aside to marinate while you prep the remaining ingredients.
      4. [    —] (  2m preheat) 
         Heat a large cast-iron skillet or heavy skillet over high heat until very hot, about 2 minutes.
      5. [ T-27] (  6m cook) 
         Add 1 tablespoon olive oil to the hot skillet, then spread the marinated chicken strips in a single layer and cook undisturbed for 3 minutes, then toss and cook 2–3 minutes more until charred at the edges and cooked through; transfer to a plate.
      6. [ T-22] (  6m cook) 
         In the same skillet over high heat, add the poblano strips, onion, and red bell pepper (or the fajita vegetable mix and poblano strips) and cook, tossing occasionally, for 5–6 minutes until the vegetables are blistered and tender.
      7. [ T-20] (  3m cook) 
         Add the 1½ cups fresh corn kernels to the skillet and cook, stirring, for 2 minutes until lightly charred; return the chicken and any resting juices to the pan, drizzle with the remaining 1 tablespoon lime juice, and toss everything together for 1 minute.
      8. [ T-14] (  2m cook) 
         Wrap the 12 flour tortillas in a damp paper towel and microwave for 45–60 seconds until warm and pliable, or wrap in foil and warm in a 300°F oven for 10 minutes.
      9. [  T-8] (  3m assemble) 
         Pile the chicken and vegetable mixture into the warm tortillas and finish with ½ cup sour cream (optional), ¼ cup roughly chopped cilantro (optional), and lime wedges for squeezing over.

  ▸ Classic Chicken Tetrazzini   [Saturday]  FLATTENED (single-dish)
    footer 108 min | scheduler 82 | card 82 | fresh derive 82 | dishes 1
      1. [ T-82] ( 22m cook) 
         Poach 1½ pounds boneless skinless chicken breast in a pot of salted simmering water for 18–20 minutes until cooked through, then transfer to a cutting board and shred with two forks.
      2. [    —] ( 15m preheat) 
         Preheat the oven to 375 °F and grease a 9×13-inch baking dish.
      3. [ T-60] (  8m prep) 
         Finely dice 1 yellow onion, mince 3 garlic cloves, and slice 8 ounces cremini mushrooms.
      4. [ T-60] (  9m cook) 
         Cook 8 ounces spaghetti (broken in half) in a large pot of well-salted boiling water for 2 minutes less than package directions, then drain and set aside.
      5. [ T-52] (  7m cook) 
         Melt 4 tablespoons unsalted butter in a large oven-safe skillet or Dutch oven over medium-high heat, add the diced onion and sliced mushrooms, and sauté, stirring frequently, for 6–7 minutes until the mushrooms are golden and any liquid has evaporated.
      6. [ T-52] (  2m cook) 
         Add the minced garlic and cook, stirring constantly, for 1 minute until fragrant, then pour in ¼ cup dry sherry and scrape up any browned bits from the bottom of the pan.
      7. [ T-45] (  5m cook) 
         Sprinkle 3 tablespoons all-purpose flour over the mushroom mixture and stir for 1 minute to cook off the raw flour taste, then gradually whisk in 1½ cups chicken broth, 1 cup whole milk, and ½ cup heavy cream, whisking constantly until the sauce is smooth and begins to thicken, about 4 minutes.
      8. [ T-43] (  2m cook) 
         Remove the pan from heat and stir in ¾ cup of the grated Parmesan, 1 teaspoon salt, ½ teaspoon black pepper, and ⅛ teaspoon nutmeg until the cheese is fully melted into the sauce.
      9. [ T-38] (  3m assemble) 
         Fold the shredded chicken, drained spaghetti, and ¾ cup frozen peas into the sauce until everything is evenly coated, then transfer the mixture to the prepared baking dish.
     10. [ T-38] (  2m assemble) 
         Toss ½ cup panko breadcrumbs with 1 tablespoon olive oil and the remaining ¼ cup Parmesan, then scatter the mixture evenly over the top of the casserole.
     11. [ T-35] ( 28m cook) 
         Bake uncovered at 375 °F for 25–30 minutes until the topping is deep golden and the sauce is bubbling around the edges.
     12. [ T-33] (  5m rest) 
         Let the casserole rest for 5 minutes before serving so the sauce firms up slightly.
