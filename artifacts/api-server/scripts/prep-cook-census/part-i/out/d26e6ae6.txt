  ▸ Classic Sauced BBQ Chicken Halves with Grilled Corn and Creamy Coleslaw   [Sunday]  SEQUENCED
    footer 77 min | scheduler 77 | card 77 | fresh derive 77 | dishes 3
      1. [ T-77] (  2m prep) Classic Sauced BBQ Chicken Halves
         Pat the 4 chicken halves thoroughly dry with paper towels.
      2. [ T-75] (  3m prep) Classic Sauced BBQ Chicken Halves
         In a small bowl, combine 2 teaspoons kosher salt, 1 teaspoon black pepper, 1 teaspoon garlic powder, 1 teaspoon smoked paprika, and ½ teaspoon onion powder.
      3. [ T-72] (  4m prep) Classic Sauced BBQ Chicken Halves
         Brush each chicken half all over with 2 tablespoons neutral oil, then coat evenly with the spice rub on all sides.
      4. [ T-68] ( 10m preheat) Classic Sauced BBQ Chicken Halves
         Preheat a gas or charcoal grill to medium heat (about 375°F), setting up a two-zone fire with direct and indirect heat.
      5. [ T-58] ( 10m cook) Classic Sauced BBQ Chicken Halves
         Place the chicken halves skin-side down over direct heat and grill 5 minutes until the skin is lightly charred, then flip and grill 5 minutes on the bone side.
      6. [ T-48] ( 28m cook) Classic Sauced BBQ Chicken Halves
         Move the chicken to indirect heat, close the lid, and cook 25–30 minutes until an instant-read thermometer inserted in the thickest part reads 155°F.
      7. [ T-46] ( 10m prep) Creamy Coleslaw
         ⟶ With the Classic Sauced BBQ Chicken Halves cooking, start on the Creamy Coleslaw.
         Thinly shred 4 cups green cabbage and 1 cup red cabbage, then peel and grate the 2 large carrots; add everything to a large bowl.
      8. [ T-36] (  4m prep) Creamy Coleslaw
         In a separate small bowl, whisk together ½ cup mayonnaise, 2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar, 1 tablespoon granulated sugar, 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard, ½ teaspoon kosher salt, and ¼ teaspoon black pepper until smooth.
      9. [ T-32] (  2m assemble) Creamy Coleslaw
         Pour the dressing over the slaw and toss thoroughly to coat every strand.
     10. [ T-30] ( 20m rest) Creamy Coleslaw
         Cover and refrigerate the coleslaw for at least 20 minutes before serving to allow the flavors to meld and the cabbage to soften slightly.
     11. [ T-29] (  4m prep) Grilled Corn on the Cob
         ⟶ With the Classic Sauced BBQ Chicken Halves cooking, start on the Grilled Corn on the Cob.
         Husk the 4 ears of corn and rub each one with ½ tablespoon softened unsalted butter, then season with ½ teaspoon kosher salt and ¼ teaspoon black pepper.
     12. [ T-20] (  5m cook) Classic Sauced BBQ Chicken Halves
         ⟶ With the Creamy Coleslaw chilling, start on the Classic Sauced BBQ Chicken Halves.
         Brush the chicken generously with 1 cup of BBQ sauce, flip, and brush the other side; cook over indirect heat 5 more minutes until the glaze is set and the internal temperature reaches 165°F.
     13. [ T-15] (  5m rest) Classic Sauced BBQ Chicken Halves
         Transfer the chicken to a cutting board and rest 5 minutes, then serve with the remaining ½ cup BBQ sauce on the side.
     14. [ T-15] ( 14m cook) Grilled Corn on the Cob
         ⟶ With the Creamy Coleslaw chilling, start on the Grilled Corn on the Cob.
         Place the corn directly over the medium-heat grill grates and cook, turning every 3–4 minutes, for 12–15 minutes total until kernels are tender and charred in spots.
     15. [  T-1] (  1m assemble) Grilled Corn on the Cob
         Transfer the corn to a platter and serve immediately.
