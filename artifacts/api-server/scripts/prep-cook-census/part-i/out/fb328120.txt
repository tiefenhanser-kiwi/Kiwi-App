         Spread a thin layer of the chicken-sauce mixture across the bottom of the prepared baking dish; layer one-third of the quartered tortillas over it, then half the remaining chicken-sauce mixture, and ½ cup each of Monterey Jack and cheddar; repeat the tortilla and chicken-sauce layers, then top with the final third of tortillas and the remaining 1 cup Monterey Jack and ½ cup cheddar.
     13. [ T-35] ( 30m cook) Spicy King Ranch Chicken Casserole
         Bake uncovered at 375°F for 28–32 minutes until the cheese is melted and bubbling at the edges and the top is lightly golden.
     14. [  T-5] (  5m rest) Spicy King Ranch Chicken Casserole
         Let the casserole rest 5 minutes, then scatter ¼ cup roughly chopped fresh cilantro over the top (optional) and serve.
