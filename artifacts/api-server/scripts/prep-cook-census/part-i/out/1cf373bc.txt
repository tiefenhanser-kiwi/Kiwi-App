  ▸ Classic Tex-Mex Sheet-Pan Chicken Fajitas   [Sunday]  SEQUENCED
    footer 41 min | scheduler 41 | card 41 | fresh derive 41 | dishes 3
      1. [ T-41] ( 12m preheat) Sheet-Pan Chicken Fajitas
         Preheat the oven to 425°F and position a rack in the upper third.
      2. [ T-41] (  4m prep) Sheet-Pan Chicken Fajitas
         In a large bowl, whisk together 3 tablespoons olive oil, 2 teaspoons chili powder, 1½ teaspoons ground cumin, 1 teaspoon smoked paprika, 1 teaspoon garlic powder, ½ teaspoon dried oregano, 1 teaspoon kosher salt, ½ teaspoon black pepper, 3 minced garlic cloves, and the juice of 2 limes.
      3. [ T-37] (  3m assemble) Guacamole
         ⟶ With the Sheet-Pan Chicken Fajitas heating up, start on the Guacamole.
         Halve and pit the 3 ripe avocados, scoop the flesh into a medium bowl, and mash with a fork to your preferred texture.
      4. [ T-34] (  2m assemble) Guacamole
         Fold in the juice of 1 lime, 1 seeded and minced jalapeño, ¼ cup chopped fresh cilantro, ¼ cup finely diced red onion, 1 minced garlic clove, and ½ teaspoon kosher salt.
      5. [ T-32] (  4m prep) Sheet-Pan Chicken Fajitas
         Add the 1½ pounds thinly sliced chicken breast, 2 sliced red bell peppers, 1 sliced green bell pepper, and 1 sliced yellow onion to the bowl and toss until everything is evenly coated.
      6. [ T-28] (  2m cook) Sheet-Pan Chicken Fajitas
         Spread the chicken and vegetables in a single layer across one or two large sheet pans, making sure nothing is overlapping.
      7. [ T-28] (  5m hold) Guacamole
         Taste and adjust salt or lime juice as needed, then press plastic wrap directly onto the surface and refrigerate until ready to serve.
      8. [ T-26] ( 22m cook) Sheet-Pan Chicken Fajitas
         ⟶ With the Guacamole chilling, start on the Sheet-Pan Chicken Fajitas.
         Roast at 425°F for 20–22 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through and the peppers and onions are charred at the edges.
      9. [ T-26] (  8m cook) Sheet-Pan Chicken Fajitas
         While the fajita filling roasts, wrap the 12 flour tortillas in foil and place them in the oven for the last 8 minutes to warm through.
     10. [ T-23] (  1m assemble) Guacamole
         ⟶ With the Sheet-Pan Chicken Fajitas cooking, start on the Guacamole.
         Transfer the guacamole to a serving bowl just before bringing it to the table.
     11. [  T-7] (  2m assemble) Fajita Toppings
         ⟶ With the Sheet-Pan Chicken Fajitas cooking, start on the Fajita Toppings.
         Set out ½ cup sour cream, 1 cup salsa, and 1 cup shredded Mexican cheese blend in individual small bowls.
     12. [  T-4] (  2m assemble) Sheet-Pan Chicken Fajitas
         Transfer the roasted chicken and vegetables to a large platter and serve immediately with the warm tortillas.
     13. [  T-2] (  2m assemble) Fajita Toppings
         Roughly chop ¼ cup fresh cilantro (optional) and cut 1 lime into wedges (optional), then arrange them alongside the other toppings for guests to add as they like.
