      2. [ T-41] ( 10m rest) Baja Chili-Lime Shrimp Tacos
         Add 2 tablespoons olive oil, the zest and juice of 1 lime, and 1½ pounds patted-dry shrimp to the seasoning bowl; toss to coat evenly and let sit for 10 minutes.
      3. [ T-41] (  4m prep) Chipotle Crema
         ⟶ With the Baja Chili-Lime Shrimp Tacos resting, start on the Chipotle Crema.
         Mince 2 chipotle peppers in adobo and finely grate 1 clove of garlic.
      4. [ T-37] (  8m prep) Tangy Cabbage Slaw
         ⟶ With the Baja Chili-Lime Shrimp Tacos resting, start on the Tangy Cabbage Slaw.
         Finely shred 3 cups green cabbage and 1 cup red cabbage and add to a large bowl.
      5. [ T-29] (  3m assemble) Chipotle Crema
         In a small bowl, whisk together ½ cup sour cream, ¼ cup mayonnaise, the minced chipotles, 1 teaspoon adobo sauce, 1 tablespoon fresh lime juice, the grated garlic, and ¼ teaspoon kosher salt until smooth.
      6. [ T-26] ( 10m rest) Chipotle Crema
         Taste and adjust salt or lime juice, then refrigerate the crema until ready to serve — it can be made up to 2 days ahead.
      7. [ T-26] (  4m prep) Tangy Cabbage Slaw
         ⟶ With the Chipotle Crema chilling, start on the Tangy Cabbage Slaw.
         Seed and thinly slice 1 jalapeño and roughly chop ¼ cup fresh cilantro; add both to the cabbage bowl.
      8. [ T-22] (  2m preheat) Baja Chili-Lime Shrimp Tacos
         ⟶ With the Chipotle Crema chilling, start on the Baja Chili-Lime Shrimp Tacos.
         Heat a large cast-iron skillet or heavy pan over medium-high heat until very hot.
      9. [ T-20] (  5m cook) Baja Chili-Lime Shrimp Tacos
         Add the shrimp in a single layer and cook 1½–2 minutes per side, until pink, curled, and lightly charred at the edges — work in batches if needed to avoid crowding.
     10. [ T-15] (  3m prep) Tangy Cabbage Slaw
         In a small bowl or jar, whisk together 3 tablespoons fresh lime juice, 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar, 1 teaspoon honey, 1 tablespoon olive oil, ½ teaspoon kosher salt, and ⅛ teaspoon black pepper.
     11. [ T-12] (  5m cook) Baja Chili-Lime Shrimp Tacos
         Transfer the cooked shrimp to a plate and wipe the skillet clean; warm the 12 corn tortillas directly in the dry skillet, 30 seconds per side, stacking them in a clean towel to keep warm.
     12. [ T-12] ( 10m rest) Tangy Cabbage Slaw
         Pour the dressing over the cabbage mixture and toss well to coat; let the slaw sit at least 10 minutes before serving so it softens slightly and the flavors meld.
     13. [  T-7] (  3m assemble) Baja Chili-Lime Shrimp Tacos
         ⟶ With the Tangy Cabbage Slaw resting, start on the Baja Chili-Lime Shrimp Tacos.
         Slice 2 avocados just before serving and roughly chop ¼ cup fresh cilantro; cut the remaining lime into wedges.
     14. [  T-4] (  4m assemble) Baja Chili-Lime Shrimp Tacos
         Build each taco: lay shrimp on a warm tortilla, top with slaw, a drizzle of chipotle crema, avocado slices, and a scatter of cilantro; serve with lime wedges.

  ▸ Al Pastor Pork Tacos with Charred Pineapple, Cotija, and Cilantro-Lime Slaw   [Saturday]  SEQUENCED
    footer 45 min | scheduler 45 | card 45 | fresh derive 45 | dishes 4
      1. [ T-45] (  5m prep) Al Pastor Pork Strips
         Slice the pork tenderloin into thin strips and pat dry with paper towels.
      2. [ T-40] (  4m prep) Al Pastor Pork Strips
         In a bowl, whisk together the 2 minced chipotle peppers, 2 tablespoons adobo sauce, 1½ teaspoons ancho chili powder, 1 teaspoon cumin, 1 teaspoon smoked paprika, 3 minced garlic cloves, ¼ cup fresh orange juice, 1 tablespoon white vinegar, and 1 teaspoon kosher salt.
      3. [ T-36] ( 10m rest) Al Pastor Pork Strips
         Add the pork strips to the marinade and toss to coat evenly; let sit for at least 10 minutes while you prepare the other components.
      4. [ T-36] (  2m preheat) Al Pastor Pork Strips
         Heat 1 tablespoon neutral oil in a large cast-iron or heavy skillet over medium-high until shimmering.
      5. [ T-36] (  6m prep) Cilantro-Lime Slaw
         ⟶ With the Al Pastor Pork Strips resting, start on the Cilantro-Lime Slaw.
         Thinly shred 3 cups green cabbage, roughly chop ½ cup fresh cilantro, and thinly slice 1 jalapeño.
      6. [ T-30] (  2m prep) Cilantro-Lime Slaw
         In a large bowl, whisk together 3 tablespoons freshly squeezed lime juice, 1 teaspoon honey, ½ teaspoon kosher salt, and 1 tablespoon neutral oil.
      7. [ T-28] (  2m assemble) Cilantro-Lime Slaw
         Add the cabbage, cilantro, and jalapeño to the bowl and toss until evenly coated; taste and adjust salt if needed.
      8. [ T-26] (  8m cook) Al Pastor Pork Strips
         Add the marinated pork strips in a single layer — work in two batches if needed — and cook for 3–4 minutes per side, turning once, until caramelized and cooked through to 145 °F.
      9. [ T-18] (  2m prep) Charred Pineapple
         Toss 1½ cups fresh pineapple chunks with ½ teaspoon neutral oil.
     10. [ T-16] (  2m rest) Al Pastor Pork Strips
         Transfer the cooked pork to a cutting board and let it rest for 2 minutes, then roughly chop into bite-sized pieces.
     11. [ T-16] (  3m prep) Warm Corn Tortillas with Cotija
         ⟶ With the Al Pastor Pork Strips resting, start on the Warm Corn Tortillas with Cotija.
         Cut 2 limes into wedges (optional) and crumble 3 ounces cotija cheese; set both aside.
     12. [ T-13] (  2m preheat) Charred Pineapple
         Heat a dry cast-iron skillet or grill pan over high heat until very hot, about 2 minutes.
     13. [ T-11] (  5m cook) Charred Pineapple
         Add the pineapple chunks in a single layer and cook undisturbed for 2–3 minutes per side until deeply charred grill marks form.
     14. [  T-6] (  5m cook) Warm Corn Tortillas with Cotija
         Warm 12 corn tortillas directly over a gas flame or in a dry skillet over medium heat, about 30 seconds per side, until pliable and lightly charred; wrap in a clean towel to keep warm.
     15. [  T-1] (  1m hold) Charred Pineapple
         Remove from heat and set aside until ready to serve.
     16. [  T-1] (  1m assemble) Warm Corn Tortillas with Cotija
         Set out the warm tortillas, crumbled cotija, and lime wedges (if using) for building tacos at the table.
