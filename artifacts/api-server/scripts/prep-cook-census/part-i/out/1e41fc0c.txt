  ▸ Carne Asada Tacos   [Sunday]  SEQUENCED
    footer 51 min | scheduler 51 | card 51 | fresh derive 51 | dishes 3
      1. [ T-51] (  5m prep) Citrus-Marinated Carne Asada
         In a bowl or zip-top bag, whisk together ¼ cup orange juice, 3 tablespoons lime juice, 4 minced garlic cloves, 2 tablespoons olive oil, 1 teaspoon cumin, 1 teaspoon chili powder, ½ teaspoon smoked paprika, ½ teaspoon dried oregano, 1 teaspoon kosher salt, and ½ teaspoon black pepper to form the marinade.
      2. [ T-46] ( 30m rest) Citrus-Marinated Carne Asada
         Add the 1½ pounds skirt steak to the marinade, turn to coat, and let it marinate at room temperature for 30 minutes (or refrigerate up to 4 hours).
      3. [ T-46] (  5m preheat) Citrus-Marinated Carne Asada
         While the steak marinates, heat a grill or cast-iron skillet over high heat until very hot.
      4. [ T-46] (  6m prep) Fresh Pico de Gallo
         ⟶ With the Citrus-Marinated Carne Asada chilling, start on the Fresh Pico de Gallo.
         Dice 3 roma tomatoes, finely dice ½ white onion, chop ¼ cup fresh cilantro, and (if using) seed and mince ½ jalapeño.
      5. [ T-40] (  3m assemble) Simple Guacamole
         ⟶ With the Citrus-Marinated Carne Asada chilling, start on the Simple Guacamole.
         Halve and pit 3 ripe avocados, scoop the flesh into a bowl, and mash with a fork to your preferred texture (chunky or smooth).
      6. [ T-37] (  2m assemble) Simple Guacamole
         Stir in 2 tablespoons lime juice, ¼ finely diced white onion, 2 tablespoons chopped fresh cilantro, and ½ teaspoon kosher salt until fully combined.
      7. [ T-35] (  2m assemble) Simple Guacamole
         Taste and adjust salt or lime, then transfer to a serving bowl and top with 2 ounces crumbled cotija cheese (optional) before serving.
      8. [ T-33] (  2m assemble) Fresh Pico de Gallo
         ⟶ With the Citrus-Marinated Carne Asada chilling, start on the Fresh Pico de Gallo.
         Combine the tomatoes, onion, cilantro, optional jalapeño, 2 tablespoons lime juice, and ½ teaspoon kosher salt in a bowl and stir to combine.
      9. [ T-31] (  5m rest) Fresh Pico de Gallo
         Taste and adjust salt, then let the pico sit for at least 5 minutes before serving so the flavors meld.
     10. [ T-26] (  1m assemble) Fresh Pico de Gallo
         Give the pico a final stir and serve alongside the tacos.
     11. [ T-16] (  8m cook) Citrus-Marinated Carne Asada
         Remove the steak from the marinade, shake off the excess, and grill 3–4 minutes per side over high heat until charred and cooked to medium (internal temperature 135°F).
     12. [  T-8] (  5m rest) Citrus-Marinated Carne Asada
         Transfer the steak to a cutting board and let it rest for 5 minutes.
     13. [  T-8] (  4m cook) Citrus-Marinated Carne Asada
         While the steak rests, warm the 12 corn tortillas in a dry skillet over medium heat for about 30 seconds per side, then wrap in a clean towel to keep warm.
     14. [  T-3] (  3m assemble) Citrus-Marinated Carne Asada
         Slice the rested steak thinly against the grain and serve in the warm corn tortillas.

  ▸ Beef Enchiladas Verdes with Roasted Tomatillo Sauce   [Thursday]  SEQUENCED
    footer 91 min | scheduler 91 | card 91 | fresh derive 91 | dishes 2
      1. [ T-91] (  8m preheat) Beef Enchiladas Verdes
         Position an oven rack 6 inches from the broiler and preheat the broiler to high.
      2. [ T-83] (  5m prep) Beef Enchiladas Verdes
         Arrange the tomatillos (cut-side down), poblano halves, jalapeño halves, onion quarters, and unpeeled garlic cloves on a foil-lined baking sheet.
      3. [ T-78] ( 12m cook) Beef Enchiladas Verdes
         Broil the vegetables 10–12 minutes until charred and softened, then remove from the oven and let cool 5 minutes.
      4. [ T-66] (  5m prep) Beef Enchiladas Verdes
         Peel the garlic cloves, then transfer all broiled vegetables to a blender with ½ cup fresh cilantro and ½ cup chicken broth; blend until smooth and season with a pinch of salt.
      5. [ T-61] ( 12m preheat) Beef Enchiladas Verdes
         Preheat the oven to 375°F.
      6. [ T-61] (  7m cook) Beef Enchiladas Verdes
         Brown 1¼ pounds ground beef in a large skillet over medium-high heat, breaking it up, about 7 minutes; drain excess fat.
      7. [ T-54] (  2m cook) Beef Enchiladas Verdes
         Stir 1½ teaspoons ground cumin, 1 teaspoon chili powder, ½ teaspoon garlic powder, 1 teaspoon kosher salt, and ¼ teaspoon black pepper into the beef and cook 1 minute more, then remove from heat.
      8. [ T-49] (  3m cook) Beef Enchiladas Verdes
         Warm the 12 corn tortillas in a dry skillet or microwave (30 seconds wrapped in a damp paper towel) until pliable.
      9. [ T-46] (  8m assemble) Beef Enchiladas Verdes
         Spread ½ cup of the verde sauce on the bottom of a 9×13-inch baking dish, then fill each tortilla with about 2 tablespoons of the beef mixture, roll tightly, and place seam-side down in the dish.
     10. [ T-38] (  4m prep) Mexican Rice
         Mince 2 garlic cloves and measure out 2 tablespoons tomato paste, ½ teaspoon ground cumin, and ½ teaspoon kosher salt.
     11. [ T-34] (  2m cook) Mexican Rice
         Heat 1 tablespoon vegetable oil in a medium saucepan over medium heat, add the minced garlic, and cook 1 minute until fragrant.
     12. [ T-32] (  2m assemble) Beef Enchiladas Verdes
         Pour the remaining verde sauce evenly over the enchiladas and sprinkle all 8 ounces of shredded Monterey Jack cheese on top.
     13. [ T-30] (  1m cook) Mexican Rice
         Stir in 2 tablespoons tomato paste and cook 1 minute until it darkens slightly.
     14. [ T-29] (  2m cook) Mexican Rice
         Add 1 cup long-grain white rice and stir to coat in the oil and tomato paste, toasting 2 minutes.
     15. [ T-27] ( 25m cook) Beef Enchiladas Verdes
         Bake uncovered at 375°F for 22–25 minutes until the cheese is melted and bubbling at the edges.
     16. [ T-27] (  3m cook) Mexican Rice
         ⟶ With the Beef Enchiladas Verdes cooking, start on the Mexican Rice.
         Pour in 2 cups chicken broth, add ½ teaspoon ground cumin and ½ teaspoon kosher salt, and bring to a boil.
     17. [ T-24] ( 18m cook) Mexican Rice
         Reduce heat to low, cover tightly, and simmer 18 minutes until the liquid is fully absorbed.
     18. [  T-6] (  5m rest) Mexican Rice
         Remove from heat, keep covered, and let the rice steam undisturbed for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork and serve.
     19. [  T-2] (  2m assemble) Beef Enchiladas Verdes
         ⟶ With the Mexican Rice cooking, start on the Beef Enchiladas Verdes.
         Garnish with 2 tablespoons chopped cilantro, dollops of ¼ cup sour cream (optional), and lime wedges (optional), then serve immediately.

    footer 46 min | scheduler 46 | card 46 | fresh derive 46 | dishes 1
      1. [ T-46] (  5m prep) 
         Dice the white onion and mince the 4 garlic cloves.
      2. [ T-41] (  5m prep) 
         Cut 1½ pounds boneless skinless chicken breasts into ¾-inch cubes and pat dry; season with ½ teaspoon kosher salt and ¼ teaspoon black pepper.
      3. [    —] (  2m prep) 
         Measure out 1½ teaspoons ground cumin, 1 teaspoon chili powder, ½ teaspoon dried oregano, ½ teaspoon smoked paprika, and ¼ teaspoon cayenne pepper (optional) into a small bowl.
      4. [ T-36] (  3m prep) 
         Cube the 4 oz cream cheese and leave it at room temperature; drain and rinse the two 15-oz cans of cannellini beans.
      5. [    —] (  6m cook)

      6. [ T-34] (  5m cook) 
         Reduce the heat to medium, add the diced onion to the pot, and cook for 4 minutes, stirring occasionally, until softened; add the minced garlic and cook 1 minute more until fragrant.
      7. [ T-31] (  1m cook) 
         Stir in the spice blend (or seasoning packet), ½ teaspoon remaining kosher salt, and ¼ teaspoon black pepper; cook 30 seconds until the spices are fragrant.
      8. [    —] (  2m cook)
