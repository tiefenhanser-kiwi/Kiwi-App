  ▸ Crispy Chicken Tenders with Oven Fries and Slaw   [Monday]  SEQUENCED
    footer 46 min | scheduler 46 | card 46 | fresh derive 46 | dishes 3
      1. [ T-46] ( 12m preheat) Oven Fries
         Preheat the oven to 425°F (shares the oven with the chicken tenders) and coat a second large rimmed baking sheet with cooking spray.
      2. [ T-46] (  4m prep) Slaw
         ⟶ With the Oven Fries heating up, start on the Slaw.
         Whisk together ⅓ cup mayonnaise, 2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar, 1 tablespoon granulated sugar, 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard, ¼ teaspoon celery seed, ¼ teaspoon kosher salt, and ⅛ teaspoon freshly ground black pepper in a large bowl until smooth.
      3. [ T-42] (  3m assemble) Slaw
         Add the 14 oz bag of coleslaw mix to the bowl and toss thoroughly until every strand is coated in the dressing.
      4. [ T-39] ( 12m preheat) Chicken Tenders
         ⟶ With the Oven Fries heating up, start on the Chicken Tenders.
         Preheat the oven to 425°F and line a large rimmed baking sheet with foil, then coat it with cooking spray.
      5. [ T-39] ( 15m rest) Slaw
         ⟶ With the Oven Fries heating up, start on the Slaw.
         Cover and refrigerate the slaw for at least 15 minutes to let the flavors meld and the cabbage soften slightly before serving.
      6. [ T-34] (  2m cook) Oven Fries
         ⟶ With the Chicken Tenders heating up, start on the Oven Fries.
         Spread the 28 oz of frozen french fries in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet.
      7. [ T-32] ( 28m cook) Oven Fries
         Bake the fries for 25–28 minutes, flipping once at the halfway point, until deep golden and crisp at the edges.
      8. [ T-27] (  3m cook) Chicken Tenders
         ⟶ With the Slaw chilling, start on the Chicken Tenders.
         Arrange the 32 oz of frozen chicken tenders in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet, then dust lightly with ½ teaspoon smoked paprika if using.
      9. [ T-24] ( 22m cook) Chicken Tenders
         Bake the tenders for 20–22 minutes, flipping once halfway through, until golden brown and cooked through to 165°F.
     10. [ T-24] (  1m assemble) Slaw
         ⟶ With the Oven Fries cooking, start on the Slaw.
         Give the slaw a final toss and transfer to a serving bowl.
     11. [  T-4] (  2m assemble) Oven Fries
         ⟶ With the Chicken Tenders cooking, start on the Oven Fries.
         Immediately season the hot fries with ¼ teaspoon kosher salt and ¼ teaspoon garlic powder (if using), toss briefly on the pan, and serve.
     12. [  T-2] (  2m assemble) Chicken Tenders
         Transfer the tenders to a platter and serve immediately alongside the oven fries and slaw.

  ▸ Tortilla Soup with Rotisserie Chicken and Classic Toppings   [Tuesday]  SEQUENCED
    footer 43 min | scheduler 43 | card 43 | fresh derive 43 | dishes 2
      1. [ T-43] (  8m prep) Tortilla Soup
         Dice the yellow onion, seed and mince the jalapeño, mince the 4 garlic cloves, and mince the 2 chipotle chiles; measure out 1 teaspoon of adobo sauce and set aside.
      2. [ T-35] (  4m prep) Tortilla Soup
         Drain and rinse the 15 oz can of black beans; pull the rotisserie chicken meat into bite-sized pieces to yield 3 cups.
      3. [ T-31] (  5m cook) Tortilla Soup
         Heat 2 tablespoons neutral oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium-high until shimmering, then add the onion and jalapeño and sauté, stirring frequently, for 4–5 minutes until softened and lightly golden.
      4. [ T-26] (  1m cook) Tortilla Soup
         Add the garlic, chipotle chiles, and adobo sauce and cook, stirring constantly, for 1 minute until fragrant.
      5. [ T-25] (  4m cook) Tortilla Soup
         Stir in 1 teaspoon ground cumin, 1 teaspoon chili powder, and ½ teaspoon dried oregano, then pour in the 28 oz can of undrained fire-roasted tomatoes and 4 cups chicken broth; bring to a boil.
      6. [ T-21] ( 15m cook) Tortilla Soup
         Add the black beans and shredded chicken, reduce heat to medium-low, and simmer uncovered for 15 minutes to meld the flavors.
      7. [ T-17] (  3m prep) Toppings
         ⟶ With the Tortilla Soup cooking, start on the Toppings.
         Stack the 6 corn tortillas and cut them into ¼-inch strips.
      8. [ T-14] (  2m cook) Toppings
         Heat 3 tablespoons neutral oil in a large skillet over medium-high until shimmering and a strip sizzles immediately on contact.
      9. [ T-12] (  6m cook) Toppings
         Add the tortilla strips in a single layer and fry, turning occasionally, for 5–6 minutes until golden and crisp; work in two batches if needed.
     10. [  T-6] (  2m cook) Tortilla Soup
         Stir in 2 tablespoons fresh lime juice, 1 teaspoon kosher salt, and ¼ teaspoon black pepper; taste and adjust seasoning.
     11. [  T-6] (  1m cook) Toppings
         Transfer the strips to a paper-towel-lined plate and immediately season with ¼ teaspoon kosher salt.
     12. [  T-5] (  3m assemble) Toppings
         Pit and slice or dice the 2 avocados just before serving; set out the sour cream, ¾ cup shredded Mexican cheese blend, lime wedges, and crispy tortilla strips in small bowls so everyone can top their own soup.
     13. [  T-2] (  2m assemble) Tortilla Soup
         Ladle the soup into bowls and scatter ¼ cup roughly chopped fresh cilantro over the top.

  ▸ Cobb Salad with Rotisserie Chicken and Bread and Butter   [Thursday]  SEQUENCED
    footer 31 min | scheduler 31 | card 31 | fresh derive 31 | dishes 2
      1. [ T-31] ( 12m cook) Cobb Salad with Rotisserie Chicken
         Bring a small saucepan of water to a boil, lower the 4 large eggs in gently, and cook 12 minutes for hard-boiled eggs.
      2. [ T-31] (  8m prep) Cobb Salad with Rotisserie Chicken
         While the eggs cook, chop the 3 romaine hearts into 1-inch pieces, halve 1½ cups cherry tomatoes, dice the English cucumber, and thinly slice the ¼ red onion.
      3. [ T-19] (  5m rest) Cobb Salad with Rotisserie Chicken
         Transfer the eggs to a bowl of ice water for 5 minutes, then peel and quarter them.
      4. [ T-14] (  3m prep) Cobb Salad with Rotisserie Chicken
         Whisk together 3 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil, 2 tablespoons red wine vinegar, 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard, 1 minced garlic clove, ¾ teaspoon kosher salt, and ½ teaspoon black pepper in a small bowl until emulsified.
      5. [ T-11] (  3m assemble) Cobb Salad with Rotisserie Chicken
         Pit, peel, and dice the 2 ripe avocados just before assembling.
      6. [  T-8] (  5m assemble) Cobb Salad with Rotisserie Chicken
         Spread the romaine over a large platter or divide among 4 bowls, then arrange the pulled rotisserie chicken, crumbled pre-cooked bacon, quartered eggs, diced avocado, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, red onion, and 4 oz crumbled blue cheese in distinct rows or sections over the greens.
      7. [  T-3] (  2m assemble) Bread and Butter
         Slice the baguette on the bias into ½-inch pieces and arrange on a board alongside 4 tablespoons softened unsalted butter for spreading at the table.
      8. [  T-1] (  1m assemble) Cobb Salad with Rotisserie Chicken
         Drizzle the dressing evenly over the salad and season with an extra pinch of salt and black pepper to taste before serving.

  ▸ BBQ Chicken Sandwiches with Slaw and Chips   [Saturday]  SEQUENCED
    footer 22 min | scheduler 22 | card 22 | fresh derive 22 | dishes 2
      1. [ T-22] (  6m prep) BBQ Chicken Sandwiches
         Pull and shred all the meat from the rotisserie chicken, discarding the skin and bones — you should have about 3–4 cups of shredded chicken.
      2. [ T-16] (  3m prep) Creamy Slaw and Kettle Chips
         In a large bowl, whisk together ⅓ cup mayonnaise, 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar, 1 teaspoon granulated sugar, ¼ teaspoon celery seed, ¼ teaspoon kosher salt, and ⅛ teaspoon black pepper until smooth.
      3. [ T-13] (  2m assemble) Creamy Slaw and Kettle Chips
         Add the 14 oz bag of coleslaw mix to the bowl and toss thoroughly to coat every strand in the dressing.
      4. [ T-11] (  5m cook) BBQ Chicken Sandwiches
         Combine the shredded chicken with 1 cup BBQ sauce in a medium saucepan and season with ¼ teaspoon kosher salt and ⅛ teaspoon black pepper.
      5. [ T-11] (  5m cook) BBQ Chicken Sandwiches
         Warm the chicken over medium heat, stirring occasionally, until heated through and the sauce is bubbling, about 5 minutes.
      6. [ T-11] ( 10m rest) Creamy Slaw and Kettle Chips
         Refrigerate the slaw for at least 10 minutes to let the flavors meld and the cabbage soften slightly.
      7. [  T-6] (  3m cook) BBQ Chicken Sandwiches
         ⟶ With the Creamy Slaw and Kettle Chips chilling, start on the BBQ Chicken Sandwiches.
         Spread 1 tablespoon softened butter across the cut sides of all 4 brioche buns and toast them in a dry skillet over medium heat for 1–2 minutes until golden.
      8. [  T-3] (  2m assemble) BBQ Chicken Sandwiches
         Pile the BBQ chicken onto the toasted bun bottoms, top each with a few dill pickle chips, close with the bun tops, and serve immediately.
      9. [  T-1] (  1m assemble) Creamy Slaw and Kettle Chips
         Serve the slaw alongside the sandwiches with the 8 oz bag of kettle-cooked potato chips on the side.
