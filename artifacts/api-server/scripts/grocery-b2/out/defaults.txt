Reply by number. Two ways to answer a line:
  "41 no"                 -> leave it NONE (H3). Nothing to do; NONE is the norm.
  "41 bone-in pork chops" -> that specific becomes the default (H2).

A line marked `?` is one this lane PROPOSES. Everything else is NONE and
needs no reply — it is listed so any of them can be overridden by number.

WHAT A DEFAULT DOES (H2): the plain name joins the default's line and is
named as the default. `chicken thighs` 2 lb + `bone-in chicken thighs` 3 lb
becomes one line reading `bone-in chicken thighs`. It does NOT touch the
other variants — `boneless skinless chicken thighs` stays its own line (H1).

WHAT NONE DOES (H3): the plain name stays generic. If a recipe ALSO named a
variety and the generic is count-sold, the generic line carries it in whole
counts (`6 bell peppers, at least 2 green`). Otherwise both stand alone.

WHY SO FEW PROPOSALS. The bar the go-ahead set is that the plain name is not
itself something a shopper can pick up. Two generics clear it, and both are
chicken: a meat case has no unqualified `chicken thighs` or `chicken breast`
bin — every pack states its cut. `ribeye steak`, `smoked sausage`,
`hamburger buns`, `kale`, `kidney beans` and the rest are all sold under
exactly their plain name, so a default would substitute where H4 forbids it.

⚠️ A TEMPTING WRONG SIGNAL, measured and rejected: 109 of the 234
generics carry NO pack at all (purchaseUnit and purchaseDisplay both null).
That looks like 'unbuyable' and is not — `arugula`, `kale`, `brown rice` and
`hamburger buns` are in that set. It measures how far the catalog's pack
backfill got, not what a store sells. No default was proposed from it.

ONE MORE THIS LANE CONSIDERED AND DID NOT PROPOSE: `corn kernels`. Its only
variants are `fresh corn kernels` and `frozen corn kernels`, and loose corn
kernels are genuinely not a thing you pick up — but picking one would be
choosing between two products H1 keeps apart, which is the substitution H4
forbids. It is listed at NONE; override it by number if that is wrong.
